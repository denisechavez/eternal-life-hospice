A Note from Aleksandra Dubina
Dear Healthcare Professional,
Conversations about hospice are often accompanied by uncertainty. Families may not know what hospice is, when it may be appropriate, or what to expect in the days ahead.
The guide provides clear and practical information that can be shared during a discharge conversation, in a physician’s office, at a care community or with a family seeking answers.
Our hope is that it brings greater clarity, confidence and comfort during an important time.
“Informed families are better prepared to make thoughtful decisions — and to receive appropriate support earlier.”
— Aleksandra Dubina, Founder & CEO

Our Approach to Care
The Eternal Standard
Dignity, defined by four pillars of care.
 
Clinical Confidence
Physician-supported care focused on comfort, symptom management and clinical coordination.
  
 
Guided Presence
Families understand what is happening, what comes next and who to call.
 
Whole-Person Comfort
Care that considers physical, emotional, social, spiritual and family needs.
  
 
Compliance-Led Care
Care delivered with integrity, privacy, accountability and responsible clinical practice.
