When you refer a patient to us, they receive more than clinical care.
Our integrative therapy program — included at no additional expense to families — extends comfort well beyond what medication alone can provide. Every modality is clinically assessed, documented in the EMR, and coordinated with the medical director.

What We Offer
Five therapies — all included, all coordinated
01  
Sound Baths
Singing bowls for anxiety, agitation, and restlessness. Especially effective in dementia.
02  
Music at the Bedside
Live or recorded, tailored to patient preference. Reduces PRN medication need.
03  
Aromatherapy
Lavender, peppermint, chamomile. Contraindication-screened against current medications.
 
04  
Pet Therapy
Certified therapy dogs. Infection-control protocols observed. Patient consent required.
05  
Volunteer Presence
Trained companionship and respite for family caregivers. No referral needed.
All modalities are clinically assessed, documented in the EMR, and coordinated with the medical director.
