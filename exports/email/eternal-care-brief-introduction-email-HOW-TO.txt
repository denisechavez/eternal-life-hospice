THE ETERNAL CARE BRIEF — INTRODUCTION EMAIL (how-to)
=====================================================
One ready-to-send HTML email that introduces The Eternal Care Brief (the new
bi-monthly care publication at eternallifehospice.com/care-brief) and its
first issue, "Hospice Is Part of Life — A Continuation of Care."
It's written to work for the whole list at once — professional care teams
and families alike.

THE FILE (in this exports/email/ folder)
- eternal-care-brief-introduction-email.html          — the email itself
- eternal-care-brief-introduction-email-HOW-TO.txt    — this file

BEFORE YOU SEND — 2 THINGS TO DO
1. PUBLISH THE CARE BRIEF PAGES FIRST.
   The email links to eternallifehospice.com/care-brief pages. If you have
   not synced the site since the Care Brief was built, those links will show
   "page not found." In Replit click Git -> Sync, wait a couple of minutes,
   then open https://eternallifehospice.com/care-brief in your browser to
   confirm it loads. (The logo and the animated photo in the email also
   load from the live site, so this same sync makes sure everything
   displays — the moving image will show as broken until you sync.)
2. REPLACE THE UNSUBSCRIBE PLACEHOLDER.
   The footer "Unsubscribe" link is a placeholder and does nothing yet.
   Every email service requires a real unsubscribe link. After you paste
   the email into MailerLite or Brevo, swap the placeholder:
   - MailerLite: change the link to  {$unsubscribe}
   - Brevo:      change the link to  {{ unsubscribe }}
   (In both editors: click the "Unsubscribe" text in the footer, edit the
   link/URL, and paste the tag above as the URL.)
   Do NOT send until this is done — it's legally required (CAN-SPAM).

HOW TO LOAD IT INTO MAILERLITE
1. Open eternal-care-brief-introduction-email.html in a text editor
   (right-click -> Open With -> TextEdit/Notepad), select all, copy.
2. In MailerLite: Campaigns -> Create campaign -> choose the
   "Custom HTML editor" (sometimes under "Start from scratch").
3. Paste the HTML, then use Preview to check it.
4. Fix the unsubscribe link (step 2 above), send yourself a test email,
   click every link in the test, then schedule or send.

HOW TO LOAD IT INTO BREVO
1. Copy the full HTML the same way (steps above).
2. In Brevo: Campaigns -> Create an email campaign -> at the design step
   choose "Code your own" / "Paste your code" -> paste the HTML.
3. Preview, fix the unsubscribe link (step 2 above), send a test email,
   click every link, then schedule or send.

TEST-SEND CHECKLIST (do this before sending to the list)
1. Confirm the moving image is live: open
   https://eternallifehospice.com/assets/img/care-brief-sound.gif
   in your browser. You should see the candle flicker and soft ripples
   from the singing bowl (about a 4-second loop that repeats forever).
   If you see "page not found," click Git -> Sync in Replit and wait a
   couple of minutes.
2. In MailerLite/Brevo, use "Send a test email" and send it to yourself —
   ideally one Gmail address AND one Outlook/Hotmail address, since they
   display email differently.
3. In each inbox, check:
   - The animation plays (Gmail, Apple Mail, and Outlook on the web all
     play it; older Outlook on Windows shows only the first frame — that
     frame is designed to look like a clean still photo, so it's fine).
   - The logo shows in the plum masthead.
   - Click every link: the photo, "Read Issue One," "Refer a Patient,"
     "Open the Family Guide," and the footer website link — each should
     open the right live page.
   - The Unsubscribe link is real (not the placeholder) — see step 2 in
     "BEFORE YOU SEND" above.
4. Only after all of that looks right, schedule or send to the list.

email also carries a hidden preheader as a fallback.)

WHAT'S IN THE EMAIL (so you can sanity-check it)
- Masthead: Eternal Life logo (loaded from the live site) + "The Eternal
  Care Brief" label, gold band, brand plum/gold/cream palette.
- Intro: what the Care Brief is — a living publication, every other month,
  to inform, support, and connect professional care teams and families.
- Issue One feature card: photo, title, one-paragraph summary, and the
  gold "Read Issue One" button.
- "In every issue" — the four topic areas (clinical guidance, family
  support, whole-person comfort, program integrity).
- Two side-by-side cards: "Refer a Patient" (for professional care teams)
  and "Open the Family Guide" (for families).
- 24/7 nurse line, then the plum footer with credentials, HQ address,
  phone, email, coverage line, and the unsubscribe placeholder.

BRAND / COMPLIANCE NOTES
- Fonts: email clients can't load Fraunces/Jost, so headings fall back to
  Georgia and body text to Arial — same approach as the email signatures.
  Colors and the logo carry the brand; don't restyle.
- Terminology: "professional care teams" and "families" only — never
  "referral partner" or "care partner." Keep this if you edit copy.
- No medical-efficacy claims and no patient information (PHI) — keep it
  that way in any edits, and never include patient details in a send.
- Coverage language stays "Serving Ventura & Los Angeles County."
