THE ETERNAL CARE BRIEF — "NEW ISSUE" EMAIL TEMPLATE (how-to)
=============================================================
A reusable HTML email for announcing EACH new issue of The Eternal Care
Brief (published every other month). The one-time introduction email has
already gone out; from now on, use THIS template every cycle — you only
swap six things and send.

THE FILES (in this exports/email/ folder)
- eternal-care-brief-new-issue-template.html          — the reusable template
- eternal-care-brief-new-issue-template-HOW-TO.txt    — this file
(The one-time intro email and its HOW-TO also live here for reference.)

EVERY NEW ISSUE — THE 6 SWAPS
Open the template in a text editor (right-click -> Open With ->
TextEdit/Notepad) and use Find (Ctrl/Cmd+F) to search for "SWAP-ME".
There are 6 numbered fields:

  SWAP-ME 1 — PREHEADER (hidden preview sentence)
    One warm sentence about this issue. It shows next to the subject line
    in the inbox. Replace the whole placeholder sentence.

  SWAP-ME 2 — ISSUE NUMBER + MONTH/YEAR (appears in TWO places)
    Example: "Issue Two · September 2026"
    Place 1: the gold kicker line at the top ("A New Issue of The Eternal
    Care Brief · ..."). Place 2: the small gold line inside the photo card.
    Make both match.

  SWAP-ME 3 — THE ISSUE LINK (appears in THREE places)
    The web address of the new issue's page, e.g.
    https://eternallifehospice.com/care-brief/your-new-issue-title
    Replace it in all three: the photo link, the title link, and the gold
    "Read the New Issue" button. Use the SAME address for all three.

  SWAP-ME 4 — HERO PHOTO
    The full live-site address of this issue's photo, e.g.
    https://eternallifehospice.com/assets/img/your-photo.jpg
    IMPORTANT: it must be a live https://eternallifehospice.com/... address
    (email programs can't show files from your computer). If the photo is
    new, sync the site first so the address actually works.

  SWAP-ME 5 — ISSUE TITLE (appears in TWO places)
    Place 1: the big plum headline inside the card. Place 2: the photo's
    alt text (the description that shows if the image doesn't load).
    You can also update the <title> tag near the top — it only affects
    the browser tab, not the inbox.

  SWAP-ME 6 — SUMMARY + READ-TIME LINE
    The one-paragraph summary (2–4 sentences: what care teams will find,
    and what's written for families), plus the small gray line underneath
    ("X sections · X min read · prints as a clean handout").

Everything else — masthead, gold band, "Browse every Care Brief" link,
the two Refer/Family Guide cards, 24/7 nurse line, and the footer — stays
exactly the same every time. Don't restyle.

BEFORE YOU SEND — 2 CHECKS, EVERY TIME
1. PUBLISH THE NEW ISSUE PAGE FIRST.
   The email links to the new issue on eternallifehospice.com. In Replit
   click Git -> Sync, wait a couple of minutes, then open the issue's
   address in your browser to confirm it loads (this also makes sure the
   photo displays).
2. REPLACE THE UNSUBSCRIBE PLACEHOLDER.
   The footer "Unsubscribe" link is a placeholder. After pasting into
   MailerLite or Brevo, swap it:
   - MailerLite: change the link to  {$unsubscribe}
   - Brevo:      change the link to  {{ unsubscribe }}
   Do NOT send until this is done — it's legally required (CAN-SPAM).

HOW TO LOAD IT INTO MAILERLITE
1. Make the 6 swaps, then select all the HTML and copy.
2. MailerLite: Campaigns -> Create campaign -> "Custom HTML editor".
3. Paste, Preview, fix the unsubscribe link, send yourself a test,
   click every link in the test, then schedule or send.

HOW TO LOAD IT INTO BREVO
1. Copy the full swapped HTML the same way.
2. Brevo: Campaigns -> Create an email campaign -> at the design step
   choose "Code your own" / "Paste your code" -> paste.
3. Preview, fix the unsubscribe link, send a test, click every link,
   then schedule or send.

SUBJECT LINE PATTERNS (swap in your issue details)
  A:  The Eternal Care Brief, Issue [Number] — [Issue Title]
  B:  [Issue Title] — the new Care Brief is here
  C:  New from Eternal Life Hospice: [Issue Title]
Preview text: reuse your SWAP-ME 1 sentence in the ESP's "preview text" /
"preheader" field.

BRAND / COMPLIANCE NOTES (same rules as always)
- Fonts: headings fall back to Georgia, body to Arial (email clients
  can't load Fraunces/Jost). Colors + logo carry the brand; don't restyle.
- Terminology: "professional care teams" and "families" only — never
  "referral partner" or "care partner."
- No medical-efficacy claims and no patient information (PHI) in the
  summary or anywhere else — ever.
- Coverage language stays "Serving Ventura & Los Angeles County."
