A Note from Aleksandra
The second patient in every room
In every home I have visited, there is always a second patient — one who does not appear on any clinical chart. The family caregiver. The person who has quietly reorganized their entire life around someone else’s illness: their sleep, their schedule, their sense of who they are outside this role.
This issue of the Care Brief is for them. And for the care professionals who see those caregivers at the bedside every time they visit — who are treating two people in every room.
“You arrived. You stayed. You’re still here. That matters more than you know.”
Aleksandra Dubina  ·  Founder & CEO, Eternal Life Hospice

Our Approach to Care
The Eternal Standard
Dignity, defined by four pillars of care.
 
Clinical Confidence
Physician-supported care focused on comfort, symptom management and clinical coordination.
  
 
Guided Presence
Families understand what is happening, what comes next and who to call.
 
Whole-Person Comfort
Care that considers physical, emotional, social, spiritual and family needs.
  
 
Compliance-Led Care
Care delivered with integrity, privacy, accountability and responsible clinical practice.
