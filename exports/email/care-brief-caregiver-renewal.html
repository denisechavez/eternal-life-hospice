Care Brief · Issue Three · August 2026
Rest, Renewal and the
Work of Caring
At the heart of every hospice visit is someone who gives. The nurse who adjusts a dose at 2 a.m. The social worker who holds a family meeting when no one else can. The chaplain who sits in silence when there are no right words. And in living rooms and bedrooms that have become care settings, the spouse, the adult child, the sibling who has quietly reorganized everything to be present.
Sustained giving, without renewal, depletes. This issue of the Care Brief is for everyone at the bedside — professional and family caregivers alike — with practical guidance on what renewal actually looks like, the signals that it has become urgent, and what hospice teams can do for each other and for the families they support.
