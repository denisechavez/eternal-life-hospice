Audit method and limitations

Exact matching ignores case, whitespace and apostrophe typography, but not hyphenation or grammar changes. Topic candidates use subject headings and explicit geography. These are indications of natural coverage, not proof: candidate explanations must be checked for depth, clinical accuracy and local usefulness. A bare city mention, first-call phrase, generic Medicare disclaimer or link alone does not establish meaningful coverage. No Google Search Console, ranking, AI-answer visibility or live production-publication evidence was queried.

The source calendar makes 23 articles date-eligible as of October 8. That is not a verified live/approved publication count. Inventory statuses state this distinction.

All 56 complete rolling 30-day windows within the supplied program are audited separately. They contain 4–6 exact phrases and 30–38 topic candidates. Fixed-cycle coverage alone does not satisfy rolling coverage. Windows outside the supplied program are not evaluated.

Claims and service safeguards

  • “Best” terms need provider-comparison criteria, not unsupported “best” or highest-rated claims.
  • 24/7 on-call guidance is not continuous bedside nursing. Same-day admissions require evaluation, certification and actual operational capacity.
  • Hospital/house/facility keywords need care-setting explanations; do not imply Eternal owns an inpatient hospice facility.
  • Standalone palliative-care affordability requires accurate comparison guidance; do not advertise an unverified Eternal service, price or insurance benefit.
  • Long Beach, Glendale and Pasadena appear in the ZIP lookup, but lookup entries are not guarantees of present capacity, affiliations or admission eligibility.
  • Branded Westlake Village reviews must identify Eternal Life Hospice correctly, not the distinct Westlake Village Hospice agency. No fabricated reviews, ratings, facility facts, statistics, testimonials or current events.
  • Sound bathing and doula support remain optional, nonclinical comfort offerings. Do not imply Medicare/Medi-Cal covers them; use the approved Eternal-funded wording.
  • Preserve Care That Honors Life, Here in Moments That Matter Most and the four pillars: Clinical Confidence, Guided Presence, Whole-Person Comfort and Compliance-Led Care.

  • Publication control is still date-based, not an authoritative Studio-approved version released at 6:30 a.m. America/Los_Angeles. Saved approval enforcement and missed-release email alerts remain unconnected. This is an existing limitation, separate from SEO editing.
  • The existing Studio keyword lens mixes Journal and email content and tracks a different small term set. Stage Two needs a blog-only 96-term cycle tracker, not a relabeled aggregate word-count panel.
  • Editorial JSON and rendered HTML must stay synchronized through the existing builder. No rebuilding, architecture replacement, robots change, sitemap change or deployment is authorized by this audit.
  • Schema checks are local structural checks, not external Google Rich Results certification. Production parity, indexing and rankings remain unverified.
