ETERNAL LIFE HOSPICE — MOO PROOF ORDER GUIDE (Referral Card 5)
=============================================================
Goal: order ONE physical proof of the referral rack card from MOO and check it by
hand before committing to the full print run. This confirms that on-screen color,
spacing, bleed/trim, and legibility all match the printed piece.

NOTE: Ordering and the hands-on review must be done by a person — it means logging
into the MOO account, uploading the file, paying for a single card, waiting for it
to arrive, and inspecting it. Everything that can be checked on-screen has already
been verified (see the DIGITAL PRE-FLIGHT section at the bottom).

----------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO ORDER THE PROOF (do this exactly)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Log into moo.com with the Eternal Life account.
2. Choose Rack Cards (the file is built to MOO's rack card template:
   bleed 3.83 x 8.66 in / trim 3.67 x 8.5 in / safe 3.5 x 8.34 in).
3. Stock / finish: Matte, heaviest weight offered (the specs call for matte,
   ~100 lb). Match whatever you intend to use for the full run so the proof is
   representative.
4. Printed both sides. Upload the CMYK PDF above (front = page 1, back = page 2).
5. Quantity: the smallest MOO allows (this is a single proof, not the run).
6. On any "color" or "file" option, keep it as "print my file as supplied."
   Do NOT enable any auto-enhance / auto-color / RGB conversion.
7. In notes to the printer (if a field exists): "Files are CMYK, bleed included,
   trim to the MOO rack-card size. Do not re-convert or auto-correct color."
8. Place the order and wait for delivery.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT TO CHECK ON THE PRINTED PROOF (tick each — front AND back)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
[ ] TRIM / BLEED: card is cut cleanly to size; no white slivers at any edge;
        the full-bleed plum (front) and cream/plum panels (back) run right to
        the paper edge with nothing important cut off.
[ ] SAFE ZONE: all text, the logo, the QR and the contact blocks sit comfortably
        inside the edge — nothing looks crowded against the trim.
[ ] COLOR: deep plum reads rich (not muddy/brown or blue-purple); gold reads warm
        and metallic-ish (not flat yellow or greenish); cream is soft (not gray).
        Compare against the swatch sheet if you brought it.
[ ] SPACING: the layout matches the on-screen design — section gaps look even,
        the credential band and footer sit where expected, nothing overlaps.
[ ] LEGIBILITY: the smallest text (address, credentials line, secure-comms labels)
        is readable at arm's length; the 24/7 phone number is easy to find fast.
[ ] QR SCAN: point your phone camera at BOTH QR codes (front + back). Each must
        open https://eternallifehospice.com/refer (the partner referral form).
        A QR that scans on screen can still fail if printed too small/low-contrast.
[ ] FEEL: hold it like a referral partner would — does it read premium and calm?

----------------------------------------------------------------------
IF SOMETHING IS WRONG
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Tell me exactly what (e.g. "plum prints too dark," "back QR won't scan," "footer
too close to the bottom edge," "gold looks greenish") and I'll adjust the source
(scripts/build-referral-card5-moo-print.py) and re-export the CMYK file. Then
re-proof only the changed card before the full MOO run.
