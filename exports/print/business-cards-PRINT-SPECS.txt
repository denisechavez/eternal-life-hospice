ETERNAL LIFE HOSPICE — BUSINESS CARDS (double-sided)
====================================================
Print at: Moo (or any digital printer). Stock: matte, heavy cover (e.g. 16pt+).
Finish: double-sided (each PDF = 2 pages: front, then back).

SIZE / BLEED (already built into the files)
- Final trim size: 3.5" wide x 2.0" high (standard US business card)
- Bleed: 0.125" on all sides (art extends to 3.75" x 2.25")
- PDF page size: 4.0" x 2.5" (pdfinfo reads 288 x 180 pts) — the extra margin
  holds the crop marks
- Crop/trim marks: 8 corner ticks printed — trim to the marks (3.5 x 2.0)
- Safe zone: all text/logo/QR kept well inside the trim
- Color: RGB, vector text. Tell the printer: "bleed is included; trim to
  3.5 x 2.0 at the crop marks."

THE FILES

- eternal-life-business-card-TEMPLATE.pdf           — blank, for new staff cards
- eternal-life-business-card-TEMPLATE.html          — editable source template

FRONT: name + title, full "Eternal / Life Hospice" lockup, contact block
(24/7 line 805.953.7273, fax, email, eternallifehospice.com, HQ address), and a
credentials + coverage footer (Medicare-Certified · CDPH-Licensed · ACHC-Accredited /
Serving Ventura & Los Angeles County).
BACK: cream brand mark, "Care That Honors Life" + "Here in Moments That Matter Most",
QR (resolves to https://eternallifehospice.com) + credentials.

>>> CONFIRM BEFORE PRINTING <<<
- Email addresses are drafts using the firstname@eternallifehospice.com convention
  (e.g. aleksandra@eternallifehospice.com). Confirm the real mailbox exists, or
  swap in the correct address, before ordering.
- Add a direct cell line if the cardholder wants one.

HOW TO MAKE MORE STAFF CARDS (quick)
Option A (template HTML): edit eternal-life-business-card-TEMPLATE.html — change the
  name, title, and email — copy it into website/elh-preview/, then render:
    chromium --headless=new --no-sandbox --disable-gpu --no-pdf-header-footer \
      --print-to-pdf=out.pdf http://localhost:5000/eternal-life-business-card-TEMPLATE.html
  Move out.pdf into exports/print/ and DELETE the temp HTML from elh-preview
  (the published site must not ship stray pages).
Option B (generator): add an entry to the CARDS dict in
  scripts/build-business-cards.py and re-run it (writes HTML into elh-preview),
  then render + move + delete as above.
QR is reusable at website/elh-preview/assets/qr-eternallifehospice.png.
