Print settings — THE ONE THAT MATTERS MOST:
  • Scale / Page Sizing: "Actual size" or "100%"  — NOT "Fit to page."
    (Fit-to-page shrinks the art and the proof will measure wrong.)
  • Paper: Letter (8.5 x 11). Each card prints onto one sheet with white around it.
  • Color: Color printer. Cream/ivory paper if you have it; otherwise plain white
    is fine for a shape/size/QR test (judge final color at Moo).
  • Double-sided: if your printer does it, great; if not, just print the fronts
    (page 1 of each) for the desk test — backs are verified on screen.

After printing, trim each card to the corner crop marks: 3.5" wide x 7.75" tall.
A ruler confirms it: trimmed card should measure exactly 3.5 x 7.75 inches.
