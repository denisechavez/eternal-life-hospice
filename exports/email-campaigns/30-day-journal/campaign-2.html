Understanding coverage and care

Medicare, hospice, and palliative care can feel like a lot to sort through. Here is a clear place to start.

Hospice is a Medicare benefit for people who meet eligibility requirements and choose comfort-focused care rather than treatment intended to cure a terminal illness. The hospice team coordinates services, supplies, medications related to the terminal diagnosis, and support for the family.

Palliative care focuses on comfort and quality of life at any stage of serious illness. It can often be provided alongside disease-directed treatment. Hospice is a specific benefit with its own eligibility and coverage rules.

Coverage note: The hospice benefit is generally covered in full with no deductible for the benefit itself. Some comfort medications may have a small copayment, and inpatient respite can involve coinsurance. Ask your hospice and plan about your situation.

Our guide explains the distinctions, questions to ask, and how a conversation with your physician can help you choose the right support.
