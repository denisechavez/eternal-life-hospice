Knowing when to call

Wondering whether it is time to ask about hospice? You do not have to wait for a crisis or have every answer ready.

A conversation can begin when a serious illness is changing daily life: more time in bed, repeated hospital visits, increasing help with eating or bathing, or symptoms that are becoming harder to manage. These signs do not make an eligibility decision—but they are meaningful reasons to ask a clinician for an evaluation.

The right time to call is often when you need clarity—not only when you need an immediate decision.

Hospice care is generally considered when a physician certifies a life expectancy of six months or less if an illness follows its usual course. A hospice team can explain options and next steps without pressure. Care decisions remain with the patient, family, and treating clinicians.
