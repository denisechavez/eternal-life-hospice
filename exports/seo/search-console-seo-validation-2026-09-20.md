## 6. Cannibalization report

1. **Evaluation/referral:** #1, #12, and #30 overlap strongly. Select one canonical guide with family and professional sections; link the others only if retained as narrowly distinct supporting content.
2. **Post-admission journey:** #1, #4, #11, #19, #20, and #21 can coexist only when each owns evaluation, discharge, first 48 hours, medication, equipment, or after-hours support respectively.
3. **Local cluster:** #3, #13–16 and the matching city/region pages must have different jobs. City pages own “hospice in [place]”; articles provide local logistics and family context. Do not make every article a location landing page.
4. **Family/caregiver:** #18, #22, #25, #29 and the caregiver/children extras need distinct stages: caregiver role, respite, refusal conversation, bereavement, and child communication.
5. **Medicare/comparison:** #5 should support the Medicare resource; #26 should own provider-selection criteria. Neither should make unsupported “best,” coverage, or outcome claims.
6. **Clinical guides:** #7–10 should remain condition-specific and avoid repeating generic signs, eligibility, or service introductions.

## 7. Recommended internal-link architecture

Use the work-order hierarchy:

`Educational blog/resource → core hospice or clinical page → county/relevant local page → referral/contact pathway`

Recommended implementation rules:

- Every retained article should have one contextual link to its core owner near the first relevant explanation and one appropriate next-step CTA.
- Evaluation/referral articles link to `/hospice-care`, `/family-guide`, `/refer`, and the county hub where geography is genuinely relevant.
- Medicare content links to the Medicare resource, family guide, hospice service page, and referral path; it should not promise coverage without factual support.
- Condition articles link to the service/evaluation path, not every city page.
- Local articles link to exactly one matching city/region owner plus the county hub when useful; avoid location-page link farms.
- The county hub links to supported city pages; city pages link back to the hub and to referral/service pages.
- The blog hub should expose a clear topic taxonomy and only deployed, indexable posts.
- Review `/blog/index.html` duplication and sitemap alignment in a later technical phase; do not change it during this analysis phase.
