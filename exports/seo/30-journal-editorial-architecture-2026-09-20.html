# Eternal Life Hospice — 30-Journal Revised Editorial Architecture

**Status:** Analysis-only, pre-publication planning pass  
**Source report:** `exports/seo/search-console-seo-validation-2026-09-20.md`  
**Source records:** `website/content/30-day-journal/batch-1.json` through `batch-5.json`  
**Scope:** Revise editorial ownership before publication. No article bodies, core pages, files, metadata, canonicals, schema, or indexing directives were changed.

## Architecture rules

1. **Core pages own acquisition intent.** Existing service, resource, county, and city pages own “hospice care,” Medicare-benefit, referral, and “hospice in [city]” searches. Journal articles explain a question, decision, care stage, or family situation and link to the owner.
2. **One article, one primary job.** Each article has one dominant search intent; secondary questions must support that job rather than create a second landing page.
3. **Local articles are supporting resources.** Articles may use a location in the title when the location changes the practical guidance, but they must not present themselves as alternate city landing pages. They should link to one matching city/region owner.
4. **Evaluation/referral separation:** #1 owns the family-facing evaluation process. #12 is merged into #1 as a family-request section. #30 remains a professional/facility referral workflow and links to `/refer`; it must not repeat the family evaluation guide.
5. **Post-enrollment separation:** #11 owns the first 48 hours; #19 owns medication management; #20 owns equipment planning; #21 owns after-hours/on-call use. None should re-explain eligibility or general hospice benefits.
6. **Condition guides stay condition-specific.** #7–10 should answer disease-specific family questions and route to evaluation; they should not become generic “signs it is time” pages.
7. **Evidence boundary:** Search Console confirms cluster/page performance, not query-to-URL attribution. Proposed owners below are editorial mappings based on semantic fit and existing page subject matter.
8. **Healthcare safety:** Do not add coverage promises, outcome claims, “best hospice” claims, response guarantees, or deterministic eligibility language. Medicare/CMS references require factual review before publication.
