## The fix list — in priority order

Work top to bottom. The first three matter most for local ranking.

### 1. Google Business Profile — highest impact
- Sign in at business.google.com with the account that manages the profile
  named **"Eternal Life Hospice."**
- Confirm the address, phone, and website match the master record above.
- **Watch for the duplicate:** an older listing **"Eternal Life Hospice, Inc."**
  still lingers in Google. Do **not** feed it new info. If it's still visible,
  use Google's "Suggest an edit → mark as permanently closed," or request a
  **merge** through Google Business Profile support, so all reviews and signals
  consolidate onto the one real listing.

### 2. Yelp — wrong name + great reviews to protect
- The listing sits at your correct HQ address but under the wrong name

- **Claim the listing** (biz.yelp.com), then **rename it to "Eternal Life
  Hospice"** and confirm the rest of the master record. This keeps those 5-star
  reviews attached to the right business instead of an orphaned name.

### 3. HCAI — the state health database (may need a filing, not a web edit)
- hcai.ca.gov lists the facility (HCAI ID **406560025**) at the **Agoura Road /
  91361** address. Because this is an official state record, correcting it likely
  requires a **filing/update with HCAI/CDPH**, not a self-service form.
- **Action:** have whoever handles your licensing/compliance submit the address
  update so the state record matches 4165 E Thousand Oaks Blvd, 91362. This is
  the source many directories copy from, so fixing it helps the rest fall in line.

### 4. Bing Places

### 5. Apple Business Connect (Apple / iPhone Maps)

### 6. Directory listings — update NAP + official domain
