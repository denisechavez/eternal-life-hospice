## What this is (plain language)

The backlink audit recommended getting Eternal Life listed in the **high-trust
hospice directories and professional associations we qualify for but aren't in
yet**. These are legitimate, compliance-safe links that strengthen local and
healthcare SEO — Google trusts a hospice more when the recognized hospice
directories and associations also list it.

This is the **"add the listings we're missing"** playbook. It is deliberately
**separate** from three other pieces of work already underway, and it does **not**
repeat them:

- **Fixing wrong details on listings that already exist** (Yelp name, Agoura Road
  address on hospice.io / hospicematch, etc.) → handled in
  `nap-citation-cleanup-2026-07-14.md`.
- **The three-domains / redirect problem** → `domain-consolidation-2026-07-14.md`.
- **Google Search Console verification & the full link report** → audit §7.

> **Honesty note on what could be done from here.** Every listing below has to be
> claimed or submitted by **the business itself**, because each one verifies you
> are the real owner (a phone call to your published number, a postcard to your
> address, or a work-email confirmation). That step **cannot** be automated from
> this project. So this playbook does the whole job *up to* that point: it tells
> you exactly which directories to add, whether each is free, whether we qualify,
> the exact page to start on, the exact details to enter, and the compliance line
> to stay on. Whoever manages the business accounts then spends ~10 minutes per
> listing doing the owner-verification click.

## Tier 1 — Free national hospice/consumer directories (do these first)

High-trust, free, and the ones families and referrers actually search. Best
effort-to-payoff.

### 1a. Caring.com — FREE basic listing
- **Why:** one of the largest, oldest senior-care directories; strong domain
  authority and consumer traffic.
- **Cost:** the **basic listing is free.**
- **Start here:** https://www.caring.com/partners/get-listed
  (or search your name at caring.com/senior-care/hospice-care first — you may
  already be auto-listed from public data and can just **claim** it).
- **Support:** listing-support@caring.com · (833) 415-0214
- **Do:** claim/create the free profile, set it to the master record, add photos,
  respond to any reviews.
- **⚠️ Compliance stop-sign:** Caring.com will pitch a **paid "partner" program
  that charges per lead / per admission (cost-per-acquisition).** For a
  Medicare hospice, paying a fee tied to referrals or admissions raises
  **Anti-Kickback / Stark** concerns. **Take the free listing only. Do not sign
  any pay-per-lead or pay-per-admission contract without your compliance
  counsel's written sign-off.** (See guardrails at the bottom.)

### 1b. HospiceDirectory.org (Hospice Foundation of America) — FREE
- **Why:** a respected, nonprofit, consumer-facing hospice locator run by the
  Hospice Foundation of America.
- **Cost:** free.
- **How:** it is **not self-serve** — it's built largely from CMS/Medicare data.
  Call HFA at **(800) 854-3402** (also hospicefoundation.org) and ask to
  **add / update / confirm** your listing. Because it pulls from CMS, making sure
  your **Medicare record (CCN B31709)** shows the correct address helps this and
  several other directories self-correct.

### 1c. GetPalliativeCare.org / CAPC Provider Directory — FREE *(only if we run a palliative-care program)*
- **Why:** the only comprehensive national palliative-care directory (Center to
  Advance Palliative Care), ~1.2M visits/year, high authority.
- **Cost:** free; **CAPC membership is *not* required** to be listed.
- **Eligibility check first:** this directory is specifically for **specialty
  palliative-care programs**. Hospice care is end-of-life palliative care, but
  this directory expects a defined palliative-care service line. **List here only
  if Eternal Life actually offers a palliative-care program** (hospital or
  community). If we're hospice-only today, **skip this and revisit if/when a
  palliative program launches** — don't claim a service we don't provide.
- **Start here:** https://getpalliativecare.org/provider-directory/ (search for
  the program; claim if present, otherwise complete the short "add a program"
  questionnaire).

## Tier 2 — Professional-association member directories (membership required)

These are **paid memberships**, not free directory forms. The directory listing is
a **benefit of joining**. Eternal Life **qualifies** as a California,
Medicare-certified, ACHC-accredited hospice — so eligibility isn't the question;
it's a **budget/ROI decision** for the owner. Both are legitimate, high-trust
healthcare links (and real advocacy/education value beyond SEO).

### 2a. CHAPCA — California Hospice & Palliative Care Association
- **What you get (SEO angle):** a listing in CHAPCA's public **"Find a Hospice or
  Palliative Care Provider Member"** directory — an in-state, on-topic authority
  link.
- **Membership type:** **Provider Membership** (for CA hospice/palliative
  organizations — that's us).
- **Cost:** dues are **tiered by organization size (census/revenue) and not
  posted publicly.** Contact CHAPCA for our tier: **(916) 925-3770** ·
  calhospice.org → "Join CHAPCA."
- **Bonus value:** compliance/clinical/billing education, CA legislative
  advocacy, regulatory updates — genuinely useful for a growing hospice, not just
  a backlink.
- **Action:** decide to join → apply as a Provider Member → once active, confirm
  the directory entry matches the master record and links to
  https://eternallifehospice.com.

### 2b. National Alliance for Care at Home *(formerly NHPCO)*
- **Important update:** **NHPCO no longer exists as a standalone.** As of **July
  1, 2024**, NHPCO merged with NAHC to form the **National Alliance for Care at
  Home** (allianceforcareathome.org). The old "NHPCO Find a Care Provider" is now
  the Alliance's **"Find a Provider"** tool — that is the current national
  member directory the audit was pointing at.
- **Membership type:** **Provider Member.** Dues are based on your **Net Patient
  Service Revenue (NPSR)**.
- **Cost:** see allianceforcareathome.org/membership/providermembers (revenue-
  based; contact them for our figure).
- **Bonus value:** national advocacy, CMS/regulatory intelligence, "We Honor
  Veterans" and "Quality Connections" programs, member communities.
- **Action:** decide to join → apply as a Provider Member → ensure the "Find a
  Provider" entry carries the master record and our official domain.

> **Which to prioritize if budget is tight:** **CHAPCA first** — it's California-
> specific, so its directory link and its advocacy are the most relevant to a
> Ventura/LA-County hospice. Add the national Alliance when budget allows.

## Compliance guardrails (always — non-negotiable)

Per the project's compliance posture (Anti-Kickback / Stark):

- ✅ **Free listings and paid *memberships* are fine.** Paying association dues or
  a flat directory fee buys visibility, not referrals — that's allowed.
- 🚫 **Never enter a pay-per-lead / pay-per-referral / pay-per-admission
  arrangement** (the "partner" upsell on lead-gen directories) without written
  compliance-counsel approval. Fees tied to referrals or admissions are the exact
  thing Anti-Kickback rules restrict for a Medicare provider.
- 🚫 **Never imply an affiliation, endorsement, or clinical/financial
  relationship that doesn't exist.** List only accreditations and services we
  actually hold (Medicare-certified, CDPH-licensed, ACHC-accredited; hospice —
  and palliative care *only if* we run that program).
- 🚫 **No medical-efficacy claims** for integrative therapies in any listing
  description.
- ✅ **Consistency:** every listing uses the master record above, and every
  "website" field points to **https://eternallifehospice.com**.
