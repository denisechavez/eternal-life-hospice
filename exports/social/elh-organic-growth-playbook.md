## The Philosophy That Changes Everything

**The reframe:** Most hospice content is about dying. Yours is about living fully until the last breath — and helping families, professionals, and communities understand that death is not a failure. It is the final act of a life. That is sacred.

The brands that win on social don't educate. They **witness**. They hold space. They say the thing everyone is thinking but no one is saying out loud. That is your lane.

Your content should make someone stop mid-scroll and think:
> *"I never thought about it that way."*
> *"I needed to hear this today."*
> *"I'm sending this to my sister."*

That's what gets shared. That's what gets followers.

## What "Real, Not AI" Looks Like

### Use your phone. Seriously.
- iPhone in portrait mode with good window light = cinematic
- Ring light behind your phone, pointed at the speaker
- No filters. No heavy LUTs. Warm, natural tones only
- Never green-screen. Never virtual backgrounds
- Film in real places: your office, a patient home (with permission), a garden, outdoors

### The "too perfect" problem
AI-looking content: symmetrical, stock-photo faces, glowing backgrounds, perfect teeth, no texture
Real content: slight imperfections, authentic pauses, natural light moving, real hands, real voices

**Your secret weapon:** Aleksandra on camera. Her face, her voice, her presence. A real founder speaking plainly about death and dignity is the most powerful content format for this topic. Nothing AI can replicate it.

## How to Film: Aleksandra Talking-Head Format

This is your #1 content format. A real person, direct to camera, plain language, 45–90 seconds.

### Setup (10 minutes)
1. Window light on your face — sit facing the window, not with it behind you
2. Phone on a tripod or propped at eye level (never looking up at you from below)
3. Clean, simple background — your desk, a bookshelf, a plant, outdoors
4. Wear your brand: deep plum, cream, or gold tones in clothing
5. No lapel mic needed — just be in a quiet room and speak clearly

### Script structure (every time)
```
[0–3 sec]  Say the hook out loud. No intro. No "Hey everyone." Just the sentence.
[3–35 sec] The body. 3–4 short points. Short sentences. Natural pauses.
[35–50 sec] The turn. "Here's what I want you to know..."
[50–60 sec] The close. One simple CTA or one memorable line to end on.
```

### What to say on camera — starting points
Pull directly from your own writing. These lines are already on your site:

> *"Family caregiving is an act of love that quietly asks everything of you — your sleep, your routines, your own appointments, sometimes your sense of who you are outside this role."*

> *"There is a reason a certain song can return us, in an instant, to a kitchen from forty years ago. Music lives close to memory and close to feeling."*

> *"You cannot pour from an empty cup."*

> *"Hospice volunteers don't arrive with a clipboard. They arrive with presence."*

These are beautiful, human sentences. Read them on camera. That's the content.

## Platform Organic Growth Playbook

### TikTok — Growth is algorithmic, not follower-based
**Reality:** TikTok shows your video to non-followers first. A brand-new account with one great video can reach 100,000 people. Your goal is **watch-through rate** (percentage who watch the whole video).

**To maximize watch-through:**
- Hook in the first 2 seconds (no intro)
- Keep videos 45–90 seconds (sweet spot for this topic)
- Use text overlays so it works on mute
- Add subtitles (TikTok auto-generates, but review them)
- End on a question or open loop ("tomorrow I'll share the one thing...")

**Hashtag strategy:**
`#hospice` (browse the hashtag — engage before you post)
`#endoflife` `#deathpositive` `#palliativecare` `#caregiver`
`#grief` `#healthcare` `#medicalmythbusting` `#deathwithdignity`
`#hospicenurse` (community — comment genuinely here)

**Comment strategy — this is non-negotiable:**
- Reply to every comment within the first 60 minutes
- Pin a thoughtful comment that adds to the conversation
- When someone comments "my dad is in hospice right now" — respond with real care, not a pitch
- Use the reply-with-video feature: a comment becomes your next video

---

### Instagram — Relationships, not broadcast
**Reality:** Instagram rewards saves and shares over likes. The metric that matters is: **did they save this?** Content they save = content they come back to.

**What gets saved:** Educational carousels, quote graphics, checklists, guides people want to reference later

**What gets shared:** Content that says what they're feeling but couldn't say, or content they want to send to a family member

**Growth tactics:**
- Post Reels 3–4x/week (highest reach format)
- Carousels (educational) 1–2x/week (highest saves)
- Stories daily — polls, questions, "did you know?" — builds habit
- Engage with hashtag communities before AND after you post
- Reply to every story reply personally

**Hashtags for Instagram:** 5 max. Precision over volume.
`#hospicecare` `#endoflifecare` `#caregiversupport` `#deathwithdignity` `#palliativecare`

---

### LinkedIn — Authority, not advertising
**Reality:** LinkedIn is starved for genuine, human healthcare content. The algorithm rewards dwell time (how long people read) and comments.

**Your authority position:**
Aleksandra is a founder, certified hospice administrator, and a woman speaking plainly about death. That is rare on LinkedIn. Own it.

**What performs on LinkedIn:**
- Personal stories ("here's what I learned from a family last week")
- Contrarian takes ("timely hospice referral improves survival — most physicians don't know this")
- Industry truth-telling ("here's why families call too late")
- Complimenting other professionals publicly (nurses, social workers, chaplains) → they comment → their network sees it

**Engagement rule:** Reply to every comment within 2 hours. LinkedIn extends the post's reach window with every comment.

**Post format:** First line is the hook (no "Sharing my thoughts on..."). Short paragraphs. One idea per post. End with one question.

---

### Facebook — Community, not content
**Reality:** Facebook reach is declining for pages, but groups and comments still work. Your audience here: families who are searching, not scrolling. They are Googling "hospice Ventura County" and landing on your page.

**Strategy:**
- Cross-post your best Instagram content
- Use Facebook's longer-form posting for detailed myth-busting
- Share blog posts directly (Facebook preview cards get clicks)
- Respond to every comment publicly AND in DMs — families in crisis reach out here

---

### YouTube — Long-term SEO asset
**Reality:** YouTube is the second-largest search engine. Someone Googling "what happens in first 48 hours of hospice" will find a YouTube video before they find a blog post. Your videos become evergreen search assets.

**What to upload:**
- Blog posts as videos (you've written the scripts — Aleksandra reads them on camera)
- "Frequently asked questions" as 5–8 min conversational videos
- Sound bath demonstration (actual footage)
- "Meet the team" style videos

**YouTube SEO:** Title as search query. Description with transcript. Chapters with timestamps. Link to blog post in description.

---

### Pinterest — Passive, evergreen traffic
**Reality:** Pinterest content lives for years. A pin you post today can drive traffic in 2028. This is your lowest-effort, highest-longevity platform.

**What to pin:**
- Infographic versions of your checklists (the Family Guide checklist, the First 48 Hours timeline, the 5 Myths list)
- Quote cards from your blog posts
- The Care Brief magazine cover
- Resource guide images

**Link every pin** directly to the relevant page on eternallifehospice.com.

## The Weekly Routine (4 hours/week total)

### Monday (30 min) — Plan
- Pick 2 posts from the 30-post bank above
- Pull the image or decide Aleksandra films this week
- Write the caption draft

### Tuesday (60 min) — Film
- Aleksandra films 1–2 talking-head videos (phone, window light, 60–90 sec each)
- No editing needed if confident; basic trimming with CapCut or Instagram's built-in editor

### Wednesday (30 min) — Post
- Post the TikTok/Reel first (highest reach potential)
- Cross-post to Instagram Reels immediately
- Schedule the LinkedIn version for Thursday 9 AM via Metricool
- Schedule the Facebook version for Thursday 1 PM

### Thursday (45 min) — Engage
- Reply to every comment on Tuesday–Wednesday posts
- Spend 15 min commenting on 5–10 posts in your hashtag communities
- Reply to every DM

### Friday (30 min) — Post #2 + Pinterest
- Post the static/carousel content (educational infographic or quote card)
- Add 3–5 Pinterest pins (existing site images + descriptions)

### Ongoing — Stories
- Post 1 Instagram Story daily (behind the scenes, poll, quote, question)
- Takes 3–5 minutes. Just open the app, shoot or upload, post.
