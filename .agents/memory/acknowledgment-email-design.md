---
name: Acknowledgment email design
description: Owner-approved letter typography constraint for Eternal acknowledgment emails.
---

Use dark grey type in acknowledgment email letters. Do not use purple type
in the letter.

Do not underline bold phone numbers or numbers presented in text blocks.
Keep telephone links clickable without using an underline.

**Why:** On October 27, 2026 the owner rejected the existing letter layout and
explicitly approved dark grey instead of purple for the letter text.
The owner also explicitly requested removing underlines from numbers when
bold or placed in a text block.

**How to apply:** Apply this constraint when redesigning or revising the email
letter body, CTA wording, signoff and supporting notices. This is not a
site-wide prohibition on purple branding. Preserve the exact approved copy,
and keep revisions unpublished until the owner approves publication.

The owner approved **Personal Correspondence** for all 14 acknowledgment
emails and explicitly authorized going live on October 27, 2026.

**Why:** The earlier full-width plum-banner letter was rejected as standard
rather than premium; this is the selected direction, not an open exploration.

**How to apply:** Preserve the light stationery, left-aligned plum logo,
restrained rules, neutral grey letter text and postal footer. Keep all approved
wording and request-specific routing unchanged when maintaining this design.
