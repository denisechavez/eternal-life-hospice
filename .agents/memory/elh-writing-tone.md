name: Eternal writing tone
description: Owner-approved brand writing standard — compliant, informative, welcoming and professional.
---

Eternal Life Hospice writing must be **compliant, informative, welcoming and professional**.
Use the owner-approved acknowledgment emails as the tone reference.

**Why:** The owner explicitly approved this tone and asked to lock it in after
approving the acknowledgment copy. This is an Eternal brand standard, not a
personal writing preference.

**How to apply:** Carry this tone into future Eternal emails, website copy,
forms and family/professional communications:

- Compliant: be accurate, protect privacy and avoid unsupported claims or
  promises of admission, eligibility, results or response deadlines.
- Informative: explain receipt, next steps and contact options in plain
  language appropriate to the request.
- Welcoming: sound warm, respectful and reassuring without pressure, sales
  language or excessive sentiment.
- Professional: use polished, precise and respectful language appropriate to
  a healthcare organization, without becoming cold or unnecessarily formal.

Do not rewrite approved copy merely to improve its tone. Preserve exact
approved language, including the care CTA, unless the owner authorizes a change.
This tone standard does not authorize publishing or sending drafts.
