**Critical rule:** First four carry "The" — acts/objects/forces. *Stillness* has no "The" — it is a state. Never add "The" to Stillness.

## Mosaic Progression (Care Brief every other month)
- Month 1: Stone alone
- Month 3: Stone + Energy (two images, gold seam)
- Month 5: Three (Stone tall-left, Energy+Word stacked right)
- Month 7: 2×2 grid (all four, gold crosshair + diamond)
- Month 9: 2×2 + Stillness as wide bottom strip
