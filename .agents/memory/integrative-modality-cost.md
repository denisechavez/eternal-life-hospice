# Integrative modality cost wording

**Rule:** Integrative / comfort modalities (sound bath, music therapy, reiki,
massage, aromatherapy, pet therapy, audiology, holistic medicine, end-of-life
doula) are **NOT covered by Medicare or Medi-Cal**. Eternal Life Hospice covers
that expense itself, so **families incur no expense**. Approved phrasings:
"at no additional expense to families", "at no expense", "families do not incur
expense", and (strongest) "covered by Eternal Life Hospice". NEVER phrase it as
Medicare/Medi-Cal covering these modalities, and avoid a bare "at no cost" that
sits next to a Medicare-coverage sentence (reads as if Medicare pays).

**Why:** User instruction + Anti-Kickback / no-medical-efficacy compliance
posture (replit.md). Saying "no cost" is accurate for families — but the reason
is Eternal's own funding, not government coverage. Conflating the two is the
inaccuracy to avoid.

**Keep separate — do NOT touch these (they are accurate and different):**
- Core Medicare Hospice Benefit is genuinely covered in full: city-page FAQ /
  schema "at no cost to the patient", the "NO COPAY / Medicare Part A & Medi-Cal"
  trust badges, medicare-hospice-benefit.html, refer.html "little to no
  out-of-pocket", blog myths post. These describe the real hospice benefit.
- Skilled/mobile *medical* services in the hospice plan of care (e.g.
  comfort-therapies.html "skilled and mobile medical services … in the hospice
  plan of care at no additional cost") ARE clinical/Medicare-covered — leave as
  "cost", they are not integrative add-ons.

**How to apply:** When adding/reviewing any mention of integrative-therapy cost
(site pages, JSON-LD, meta/OG, llms.txt, chat.js system prompt, exports), state
Eternal covers it at no additional expense to families; never imply Medicare or
Medi-Cal pays. The homepage #modalities blurb ("covered by Eternal Life
Hospice") and sound-bath.html sb-note ("not covered by the Medicare hospice
benefit. Eternal Life Hospice provides them…") are the canonical templates.
