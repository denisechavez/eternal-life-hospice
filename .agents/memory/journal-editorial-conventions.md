---
name: Journal editorial conventions
description: Durable editorial and archival rules for Eternal Journal content.
---

Eternal Journal copy follows the house convention of avoiding Oxford commas. Never use “overwhelm” or any variation in Eternal Life Hospice content. Infographics are selective supplemental assets only when a comparison, process, timeline, checklist or decision path is materially clearer visually. Journal articles must support their designated core service, resource or location page rather than compete for its primary search intent. Content retained only for archival or consolidation reference must remain available as source material but excluded from all public-generation surfaces.

**Why:** Editorial approval established these language and visual-content boundaries to keep family-facing copy calm and to prevent unnecessary graphics. The archival rule keeps source material without accidentally publishing it.

**How to apply:** Use the language conventions in future Journal writing and revisions. Give every article a narrower job than its core owner and link the article to that owner; local articles should answer a distinct local question rather than restate the city or regional service page. Recommend an infographic only when the specified information genuinely benefits from visual explanation. When archiving a planned article, preserve its editorial source while excluding it from generated pages, manifests, archive cards, structured data, sitemaps and search indexes.

New Journal articles need unique imagery: photographs or convincingly photoreal
images that do not look AI-generated.

**Why:** The user explicitly required unique, credible-looking photographs for
the continuation through December 31, 2026.

**How to apply:** Prefer licensed real photographs and do not reuse an article
hero across the new collection. Retain source and license records internally.
The owner explicitly requested keeping every existing image unchanged while
removing all visible photo-credit notes throughout the website, not just the
optional Pexels notes. Do not substitute photographs to remove their credits.
Keep source and license records internally. This display instruction does not
waive license obligations or establish that an image is attribution-free.

Apply this presentation rule to served older release snapshots as well as new
drafts, without rewriting frozen documents or renewing approval records.

**Why:** The owner clarified the all-notes scope after being offered replacement
photographs and explicitly said to keep the images unchanged.
Cleaning draft files alone leaves older published snapshots visibly unchanged.

Preserve the established Journal image presentation when adding new articles.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested that new imagery retain the previous
format and that the existing treatment not change.

**How to apply:** Reuse the existing hero layout, cropping rules, overlay and
headline placement. Do not remove the purple overlay or redesign the image
treatment without approval. Distinguish unchanged template styling from
different source-photo proportions when checking consistency.
