---
name: Journal editorial conventions
description: Durable editorial and archival rules for Eternal Journal content.
---

Eternal Journal copy follows the house convention of avoiding Oxford commas. Content retained only for archival or consolidation reference must remain available as source material but excluded from all public-generation surfaces.

**Why:** The final Batch 1 editorial gate explicitly established the punctuation convention and required a consolidated article source to remain available without being scheduled, indexed or rendered publicly.

**How to apply:** Use the convention in future Journal writing and revisions. When archiving a planned article, preserve its editorial source while excluding it from generated pages, manifests, archive cards, structured data, sitemaps and search indexes.
