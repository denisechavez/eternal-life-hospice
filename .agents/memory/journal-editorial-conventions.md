---
name: Journal editorial conventions
description: Durable editorial and archival rules for Eternal Journal content.
---

Eternal Journal copy follows the house convention of avoiding Oxford commas. Never use “overwhelm” or any variation in Eternal Life Hospice content. Infographics are selective supplemental assets only when a comparison, process, timeline, checklist or decision path is materially clearer visually. Content retained only for archival or consolidation reference must remain available as source material but excluded from all public-generation surfaces.

**Why:** Editorial approval established these language and visual-content boundaries to keep family-facing copy calm and to prevent unnecessary graphics. The archival rule keeps source material without accidentally publishing it.

**How to apply:** Use the language conventions in future Journal writing and revisions. Recommend an infographic only when the specified information genuinely benefits from visual explanation. When archiving a planned article, preserve its editorial source while excluding it from generated pages, manifests, archive cards, structured data, sitemaps and search indexes.
