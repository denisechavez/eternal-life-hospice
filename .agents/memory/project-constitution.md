name: ELH Project Constitution
description: Strategic priorities and decision-making rules for all ELH work — referrals and admissions come first.
---

## The Primary Objective
Generate qualified hospice referrals that convert into admissions.
Every technical, design, and marketing decision must serve this objective.

## Business Reality
- Current census is extremely low. Cash flow is top priority.
- Marketing must produce measurable ROI.
- Do not recommend features because they are visually impressive.

## The Internal Rule
Before any recommendation, ask: **Will this increase referrals or admissions?**
If not — it is not a priority.

## Project Constitution (conflict resolution)
- Aesthetics vs. conversion → **choose conversion**
- Complexity vs. usability → **choose usability**
- Adding a feature vs. increasing referrals → **prioritize referrals**

## What to Recommend (increases priority)
- Referral volume, admissions, phone calls, form submissions
- Provider engagement, search visibility, AI discoverability
- Local authority, Google rankings, conversion rate

## Website Philosophy
The website is a referral-generation platform, not a brochure.
Every page must answer: Why Eternal? Why now? What happens next? How do I refer? How quickly will someone respond? Why should I trust Eternal?

## Content Philosophy
Every piece of content must accomplish at least one: educate, build trust, increase authority, improve SEO, improve AI retrieval, increase referrals, improve conversion, support providers, support families.

## SEO Philosophy
Every page supports: Google Search, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude, AI search, schema, structured data, internal linking, topical authority, entity optimization, medical authority.

## Marketing Stack
A professional PPC agency manages Google Ads, Microsoft Ads, Meta Ads.
Social → PPC → website → referrals. Everything works as one system.

## Email Strategy
Educational, never overly promotional. Build long-term trust. Become the preferred educational resource for referral partners.

## CRM / Reporting Philosophy
Every referral source tracked. Every call, form, admission attributable to its source.
Dashboards: referral source, admission source, campaign ROI, cost per referral, cost per admission, referral partner activity, website conversion, email/social engagement.
Weekly + monthly reporting. Never guess. Always use data.
