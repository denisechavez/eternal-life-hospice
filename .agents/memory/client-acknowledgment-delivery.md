---
name: Client acknowledgment delivery boundary
description: The owner excludes Brevo from the delivery path for client acknowledgment emails.
---

Client acknowledgment emails must not be sent through Brevo.

**Why:** The owner explicitly stated, “I do not want the e-mail that goes to client to go through brevo.” Form processing in Replit and the service delivering an email must be explained separately; a WhatConverts lead notification is not the approved customer acknowledgment.

**How to apply:** Confirm an approved replacement delivery path before enabling it, preserve the approved branded copy and layout, and test without creating duplicate intake requests. This instruction does not authorize removing Brevo from newsletters, marketing or internal notifications, or disabling WhatConverts tracking. Keep changes in preview until publishing is approved. Do not describe an installed Gmail connection as an approved Eternal sender until the company mailbox and permissions are verified.
