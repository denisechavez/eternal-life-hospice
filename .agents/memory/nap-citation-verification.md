# NAP citation verification — off-platform listings

Off-platform listing edits (Google/Yelp/HCAI/directories) are done in the user's
own accounts and take time to publish; verify by live web-fetch of the public view.

## Yelp "Westlake Village Hospice" is a DIFFERENT company — do NOT rename it
The backlink audit assumed the Yelp listing "Westlake Village Hospice" (5.0★,
6 reviews) was ELH filed under a wrong name. **It is not.** It's a separate real
hospice: own site `westlakevillagehospiceinc.com`, own phone (805) 870-0103 (not
ELH's 953-7273), own Greater Conejo Valley Chamber membership, own separate BBB
profile, established 2015.
**Why:** claiming/renaming another business's Yelp listing would be taking it over.
**How to apply:** treat the "rename Yelp" step in the cleanup guide as retired.
ELH appears to have no Yelp listing of its own — creating a fresh one is the clean
option, not renaming Westlake Village Hospice.

## Directories inherit HCAI's address — fix the state record first
As of mid-2026 the state record (HCAI ID 406560025) and the hospice directories
(hospice.io, hospicematch, hospicecarenow) all still showed the OLD **30941 Agoura
Rd, Suite 112, 91361** address, not the correct 4165 E Thousand Oaks Blvd, 91362.
hospice.io also shows a garbled phone "(80-5) -953-7273".
**Why:** the directories copy CMS/HCAI source data, so they keep re-inheriting
Agoura Rd until the upstream state record flips (a CDPH/HCAI filing, not a web edit).
**How to apply:** prioritize the HCAI/CDPH filing; directory self-service edits
won't fully settle until it updates.

## What can't be verified from outside
Google Business Profile duplicate-closure/merge, Bing Places, and Apple Business
Connect claim/edit status are only visible when signed into those accounts — the
public search view isn't enough to confirm them.
