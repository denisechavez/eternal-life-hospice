name: Physicians & Referrals CTA
description: The canonical destination for provider referral links in the shared site header.
---

Physicians & Referrals links should open the referral page at the Send a referral card, where the Tell us how to reach you form is immediately visible.

**Why:** Referring providers should be able to start the intake form without scanning or scrolling through the referral page first.

**How to apply:** Use `/refer#referral-form` for both desktop and mobile shared-header Physicians & Referrals links. Keep family Request Care links and direct phone links separate.
