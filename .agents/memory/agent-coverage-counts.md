---
name: Agent-facing coverage counts
description: Why public agent documentation avoids fixed city totals and distinguishes published pages from confirmed care availability
---

Do not publish a fixed city total as an authoritative service-area claim in agent-facing documentation without operations, clinical, and brand approval. Describe the live list endpoint's total as the number of published city-page records returned, and direct address-level coverage inquiries to the intake team.

**Why:** Published city-page records can change independently of clinical service availability. A stale numeric example understated the published list, while an unapproved replacement could be read as a guarantee of care in every listed location.

**How to apply:** When changing AI-facing coverage documentation or examples, favor a dynamic response contract over a sampled "full list." If a numeric count is needed, obtain sign-off and validate it against the published-city source before release.
