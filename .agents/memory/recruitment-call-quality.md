name: Recruitment call quality
description: Why public volunteer and career promotion is being withdrawn during PPC campaigns.
---

The user wants volunteer and career recruitment removed from the public website
and referral cards for now because PPC campaigns continue to generate calls for
those requests instead of patient-care inquiries.

**Why:** These recruitment calls interfere with the site's referral and admission
objective. This is a temporary marketing-scope request, not authorization to
eliminate the actual volunteer program or destroy its source material.

The reversible public takedown was explicitly approved on 2026-10-12. Preserve
source material and approval history, but withdraw recruitment pages, promotional
sections and discovery links from public responses. A withdrawn recruitment
article may be taken offline without deleting its original or changing its date.

**Why:** Keeping originals makes restoration possible and avoids rewriting
historical approvals. Temporary unavailable responses use 404 plus noindex;
do not block these URLs in robots.txt, since Google must see the removal status.
Existing Google results can remain until recrawled after the approved publish.

**How to apply:** Use the approved reversible takedown and inspect recruitment
pages, related resources/articles, shared chrome, search/discovery, generators,
card links and QR destinations. Publication still requires separate approval.
Keep accurate clinical descriptions
distinct from recruitment promotion. Do not use broad nursing-related PPC
exclusions that would suppress the approved hospice-nursing family-care strategy.
