# Strategic objective: get the agency on track to SELL

The user/operator runs **Conduit International** (concierge marketing consultancy;
works "behind the curtain"), engaged by the founder/CEO to get Eternal Life
Hospice **acquisition-ready** — build enterprise value toward a **$11M+ sale or
transition**, not just grow patient volume for its own sake.

**Engagement shape:** 3-month foundation build → a deliberate **Month-3 pause** to
read real numbers and formalize the working agreement (**retainer paired with
equity**); then plan 6/9/12/18/24-month horizons (24-mo aligns to the sale
timeline). A **mutual NDA** goes in place before any financials are exchanged.
Baseline metrics that seed the future sale decks: ADC month-by-month, revenue/P&L,
payer mix, ALOS + admits/discharges, referral-source conversion, Medicare cap
status, operating cost per patient day.

**Open items to reconcile before acting (confirm with user, do not assume):**
- **Pillar count:** all printed collateral shows **FOUR** pillars (Clinical
  Confidence · Guided Presence · Whole-Person Comfort · Compliance-Led Care), but
  the engagement brief references "**five pillars**" once. Confirm before laddering
  messaging.
- **Domains:** live site is **eternallifehospice.com**, but the brief also names
  **eternalhospice.com** (website soft-launch target) and **eternallifehospiceinc.com**,
  plus an "off-brand site" to take down. Confirm the canonical domain before any
  domain/email/SEO/redirect work.
- **Container Chiq** = a separate potential partnership, deliberately **tabled** to
  the Month-3 checkpoint; do not act on it.

**Immediate mandate (overrides everything short-term): GET CENSUS UP FAST.**
Engagement is a **3-month retainer**; after 3 months they decide on **% equity of
the sale**. Mutual test phase — early, visible census wins matter most right now.
Founder runs **two agencies**, but the work here is **focused only on Eternal**.
Fastest census lever = referral engine + zero-friction intake + working the warm
network first; community/press are secondary until census is moving.

**Why this matters for every decision:** hospice acquirers pay multiples on
durable, transferable value — **stable census, durable referral relationships
(hospitals/SNFs/physicians), clean compliance, and a defensible brand/
differentiation.** So work should ladder up to those value drivers.

**How to apply:**
- Prioritize the **referral-partner engine** (B2B → referral lists) — it is the
  biggest lever on both revenue and sale multiple.
- Differentiation thesis = **The Eternal Standard four pillars + integrative
  modalities (sound healing / sound bath)**; this is what makes the brand
  memorable and harder to commoditize. The sound-healing idea originated as the
  "Sound in Peace" pitch concept and now lives as the production Sound Bath page.
- Keep compliance spotless (Medicare/CDPH/ACHC; Anti-Kickback/Stark) — diligence
  risk kills deals.
- Favor systems/assets that transfer to a buyer (documented brand, collateral,
  repeatable referral process) over founder-dependent tactics.
