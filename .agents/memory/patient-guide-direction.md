---
name: Patient guide design audience
description: Audience and visual direction for the printed patient and family guide, distinct from the web family-guide booklet.
---

The printed patient guide is for patients' families. It should feel premium and sophisticated while remaining relatable, warm and connected. Nature and professionally lit photography are welcome.

**Why:** The owner explicitly identified families as the audience and requested a stronger sense of connection than the initial cover concepts provided.

**How to apply:** Evaluate concepts as a family resource to keep at home, not a clinical sales brochure. Carry the visual identity through the inside covers. Proposed founder-message copy remains a review draft until explicitly approved; do not present drafted wording as an authentic quotation or imitate a handwritten signature.

A complete cover set has four distinct surfaces: front, inside front, inside back and outside back. Label each explicitly; an inside-back contact page is not a substitute for an outside back cover.

**Why:** The owner expected the actual back cover when reviewing the cover-and-insides concept.

Apply the four-surface requirement to every concept, not just the selected option. Alternative concepts must differ in composition and hierarchy across the set, not merely swap photographs into the same template. The founder welcome on the inside front should include the existing real founder portrait.

**Why:** The owner explicitly flagged missing outside backs in the other options and said the concepts looked the same, then requested the founder's photograph inside.

**How to apply:** Compare concepts at canvas overview scale before presenting. Confirm the front covers and interior organization differ visibly, and verify all four surfaces for each option. Use the real portrait without fabricating a likeness.

The supplied table of contents is reference for framing the founder welcome, not authorization to redesign the contents page. Frame the note as an introduction to a practical hospice handbook, including team support, comfort care, safety, privacy and patient rights, rather than a general promotional welcome.

**Why:** The owner explicitly clarified the purpose of the contents-page reference.

The selected cover prints flat at 17 × 11 inches and folds to 8.5 × 11 inches. Review flat artwork as two spreads: outside back on the left/front on the right, then inside front on the left/inside back on the right.

**Why:** The owner specified a 17 × 11-inch printed piece and asked for the cover surfaces to be stitched together.

**How to apply:** Preserve the approved artwork when assembling spreads. Distinguish an actual-size review proof from a press-ready file; printer bleed, color requirements and final content approval still need confirmation.
