---
name: Service-page editorial decisions
description: Owner-approved service-brief wording exceptions and approval boundaries.
---

Omit “Phone access does not mean a nurse stays continuously at the bedside.”
from the 24/7 Nurse Access service-page introduction.

**Why:** The owner explicitly removed this sentence while approving the other
recommended service-brief revisions.

**How to apply:** Use the approved introduction describing phone support,
assessment and clinically needed visits. This is a wording decision, not a
claim that continuous bedside nursing is generally provided. Do not generalize
the removal into deleting clinical distinctions from unrelated existing pages.
The approval does not waive the brief's outstanding clinical, billing or
operational checks and does not authorize publication.

Service-page photographs should not carry visible illustrative-scene footnotes
or caption overlays.

**Why:** The owner explicitly requested removing these notes while refining
the Services overview's appearance.

**How to apply:** Keep service imagery clean and retain truthful descriptive
alt text. Removing an overlay is not permission to represent generated scenes
as actual Eternal patients or staff.

Do not show the Home / Services breadcrumb bar on the Services overview.

**Why:** The owner requested removing this strip as part of the overview's
visual refinement.

**How to apply:** Let the overview hero follow the shared header directly.
This request does not remove breadcrumbs from individual service detail pages.

Service-page photographs should feel naturally integrated into the page,
not like shaped cutouts.

**Why:** The owner rejected the cutout appearance of the arched photograph
and asked to compare alternative treatments on the canvas first.

**How to apply:** Preserve lifelike color and recognizable people when
exploring framing, full-bleed placement or an edge blend. Present alternatives
for selection before applying a new treatment to the website. A canvas
comparison does not authorize a website rollout or publication.
