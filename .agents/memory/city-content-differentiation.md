**Why:** Automated editorial review of this rollout proposed removing valid coverage and service-limit disclaimers on the mistaken premise that they were guarantees.

**How to apply:** Inspect exact proposed changes before applying them, especially coverage, treatment-election, eligibility and address-availability wording.
