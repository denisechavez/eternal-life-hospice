---
name: Insurance coverage visibility
description: Why insurance information must stay visible and easy for families to find.
---

Keep insurance and coverage options visible without requiring a family to
expand an FAQ or open the marketing kit.

**Why:** On October 27, 2026, the owner reported that even she could not find
the insurance information while showing the website to a friend and explicitly
asked for the insurers to be listed on the website.

**How to apply:** Preserve a clear homepage route to the coverage information.
Use the approved marketing materials as the reference for listed organizations,
but do not interpret a listing as proof of every product's acceptance or of an
in-network contract. Keep plan-specific eligibility, authorization and LOA
qualifications clear. Do not add third-party logos or affiliation claims merely
because an organization appears in the coverage list.
