About This Publication

The Eternal Care Brief is published every other month by Eternal Life Hospice — education, guidance, and support for the physicians, discharge planners, case managers, and social workers we work alongside, and for the families they serve. Every issue follows the same five-section format, written to educate and support both care professionals and families with sacred care in the continuum of life.

Every issue opens with a professional voice

Issue One is introduced by Aleksandra Dubina, Founder & CEO of Eternal Life Hospice and Certified Hospice Administrator. If you are a physician, nurse, case manager, social worker, or chaplain who would like to introduce a future issue, we would welcome your voice.
