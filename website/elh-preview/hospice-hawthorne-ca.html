When to consider hospice

Hospice is appropriate when a patient and their physicians agree that comfort is the right priority. Signs that a conversation may be timely include:

  • Increased hospitalizations or emergency visits
  • Progressive functional decline despite ongoing treatment
  • Growing need for daily personal care assistance
  • Uncontrolled pain, breathlessness or distressing symptoms
  • Unexplained weight loss or reduced appetite
  • Significant caregiver burden within the household
  • A shift in goals toward comfort and quality of remaining time

Eligibility requires a clinical evaluation and physician certification. Call 805.953.7273 and we will guide you through the process clearly.

Does Eternal Life Hospice serve Hawthorne?

Yes. We provide Medicare-certified, ACHC-accredited hospice care to families in Hawthorne and surrounding Los Angeles County communities — at home, in assisted living, or in a skilled nursing facility. Call 805.953.7273.

Where can hospice care be provided in Hawthorne?

Hospice care is delivered wherever the patient calls home — in private residences, assisted-living communities, residential care homes, board-and-care facilities, and skilled nursing facilities throughout Hawthorne and nearby Inglewood, Lawndale and Gardena.

How does a family in Hawthorne request a hospice evaluation?

Call 805.953.7273 at any time. Our team will answer your questions and arrange a clinical evaluation — often on the same day when clinically appropriate.

What does Medicare generally cover for hospice care?

Medicare Part A covers hospice care for eligible patients, including nursing visits, comfort medications, medical equipment, aide services, social work and chaplain support. Most covered services have little to no out-of-pocket cost; limited copayments may apply. Learn about what hospice covers.

Can a physician or care facility in Hawthorne refer a patient?

Yes. Physicians and care coordinators in Hawthorne can submit referrals at eternallifehospice.com/refer or by calling 805.953.7273.

The Eternal Standard is the four-pillar framework that guides every patient interaction: Clinical Confidence, Guided Presence, Whole-Person Comfort and Compliance-Led Care. Every family in Hawthorne receives care built on these principles — clear communication, skilled clinical support, integrative comfort therapies and ethical, transparent operations.
