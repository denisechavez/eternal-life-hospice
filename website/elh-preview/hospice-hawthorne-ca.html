Coverage and Care Logistics

Hospice election under the Medicare benefit covers services related to the terminal prognosis, including nursing, aide support and medical supplies as deemed necessary by the care plan. It is essential to understand that this benefit generally does not cover room and board, nor does it guarantee specific visitation times or 24-hour supervision.

While some medications and equipment may be covered, coverage is not universal for all patient needs. Discuss your specific situation with our team to understand which expenses fall under the hospice umbrella and which remain the responsibility of the family or other insurance sources. We strive for transparency regarding these financial details.

Clinical eligibility must be recertified periodically and services are always adjusted based on the patient's assessed need. Do not assume all previously provided treatments are automatically covered or required to stop; we will coordinate with your attending physician to determine which components of your current care plan best support your comfort-focused goals.

Common questions about hospice in Hawthorne

Will your team be able to enter my building if I am away?

Our clinicians follow established safety and building protocols for access. We generally coordinate visits when a family caregiver or representative is available to facilitate entry, as this ensures we can provide care safely and communicate effectively regarding the patient's current status and comfort needs.

Do you offer emergency access if I cannot reach someone?

Hospice is not an emergency medical service and we do not hold keys or provide security services for patient homes. We coordinate scheduled visits based on the patient's care plan. For immediate emergencies, always utilize your local emergency services rather than waiting for a hospice clinician.

Can you change your visit time if my building access changes?

We strive to be flexible but must operate within the schedules of our clinical team. If your building access changes or you have new instructions, please notify us as far in advance as possible so we can attempt to accommodate the changes without disrupting planned care delivery.
