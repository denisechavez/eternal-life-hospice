# Eternal Life Hospice — Coverage & Pricing

## Cost to families
Most hospice services provided through Eternal Life Hospice have little to no out-of-pocket cost for patients covered by Medicare Part A or Medi-Cal (California Medicaid). Limited copayments may apply for certain services such as respite care or some medications. The care team verifies coverage and handles all insurance coordination at no cost to the family.

Private insurance plans typically cover hospice under similar terms.

## What is covered
- Physician oversight and nurse case management (visits 7 days/week)
- Medications related to the terminal diagnosis
- Medical equipment and supplies
- Home health aide and homemaker services
- Social work, chaplaincy, and bereavement counseling
- 24/7 on-call nursing

## Integrative therapies
Music therapy, sound bath sessions, and other integrative modalities are provided by Eternal Life Hospice at no additional expense to families. These are complementary care services offered for patient comfort and wellbeing; they are not billed to Medicare or Medi-Cal separately and are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition.

## Eligibility
A physician certifies that the patient has a life-limiting illness with a prognosis of six months or less if the illness follows its expected course. Patients may continue receiving hospice as long as they continue to meet eligibility criteria.

## Important note
Medicare coverage details are subject to change. Confirm current eligibility and benefit details with your care team or call 1.800.MEDICARE (1.800.633.4227).
