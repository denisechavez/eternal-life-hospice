Coverage and Treatment Benefits

Under the Medicare hospice benefit, services related to the terminal diagnosis are covered to prioritize comfort. This includes clinical visits, nursing support and medications meant for symptom relief. It does not mean that all existing treatments or non-hospice-related care must cease immediately; we will help you clarify what is covered by hospice.

Families should be aware that hospice does not replace primary care or facility-based support, but rather acts as an additional layer of comfort-centered care. We help you coordinate this so that your family can focus on what matters most, rather than the logistics of insurance and medical coverage.

For detailed information on how these benefits work, we invite you to review our Medicare Hospice Benefit page. We are committed to transparency and are available to discuss any financial or coverage questions specific to your family's situation in Glendora.

Common questions about hospice in Glendora

Does choosing hospice mean all treatments must stop?

No. Electing hospice does not automatically mean every existing treatment must end. We work with the patient's physician to evaluate which treatments contribute to the patient's comfort goals. The plan of care is individualized, ensuring that necessary support continues while focusing on relieving pain and managing symptoms.

How do you coordinate with our current physician?

We maintain regular communication with the patient’s treating physician to ensure the plan of care remains aligned with the patient's comfort goals. Hospice is a supportive benefit and our team acts as a bridge to ensure clinical treatments and hospice support work together effectively in the home setting.

Are all medications covered by hospice?

The Medicare hospice benefit covers medications that are related to the terminal diagnosis and are intended for comfort or symptom management. Other medications may still be covered under your regular insurance or pharmacy plans. We help you navigate these distinctions during your initial assessment and throughout the care journey.
