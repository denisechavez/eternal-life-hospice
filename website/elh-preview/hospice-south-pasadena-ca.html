Coverage and Practical Expectations

The Medicare hospice benefit covers essential services related to the terminal diagnosis, including clinical visits, nursing oversight and medications related to symptom control. It is important to remember that this benefit generally does not cover room and board, nor does it guarantee all medications or non-hospice related health expenses.

Families often wonder what remains covered when they elect hospice. We encourage you to clarify which existing treatments remain part of your plan. Our team can help you understand these nuances during our assessment process so that expectations remain clear from the beginning.

Copayments may apply under certain circumstances. We recommend reviewing your specific insurance or Medicare documents carefully. Always direct questions regarding your ongoing coverage or specific financial responsibilities to our administrative team as you prepare for the evaluation.

Guidance and Next Steps

Understanding the difference between hospice and private-duty care is essential. Hospice provides specialized clinical visits focused on symptom management, whereas private-duty help is typically task-oriented and daily in nature. Knowing these distinctions helps you determine the right support structure for your home environment.

We provide tools to help you navigate this transition, including our Pain and Symptom Management guide. These resources are designed to help you organize your questions and prepare for discussions with our team about the care plan.

Common questions about hospice in South Pasadena

How often does the care team review the care plan?

The care plan is reviewed systematically by our interdisciplinary team to ensure it remains current. We also conduct informal reviews during clinical visits to address immediate changes. We encourage families to initiate discussions during these visits whenever they notice shifts in the patient's condition or comfort level.

What if my loved one has a personal caregiver already?

Hospice is designed to coordinate with existing support systems, including personal caregivers or family members. Our clinical team focuses on the medical and palliative aspects of care, while the existing caregivers continue to provide daily living support. We work together to ensure a comprehensive and balanced approach.

How do I know if hospice is the right choice now?

Choosing hospice is a clinical decision based on a physician's certification that a patient has a life-limiting illness and would benefit from palliative care. If you are unsure, we recommend calling us to discuss the symptoms you are seeing so we can guide you toward a formal evaluation.
