Beginning Care and Ongoing Adaptation

Beginning hospice care in Northridge starts with a comprehensive assessment of your loved one's current physical and functional abilities. We understand that these abilities change over time and our service is designed to adapt. We do not provide static care; rather, we work with you to update the individualized plan based on clinical reassessments.

During each visit, our staff observes how the patient is managing their daily tasks and symptoms. We discuss these observations with your family and the physician team to determine if adjustments to the care plan are necessary. This proactive cycle helps our support remain relevant, helpful and consistent with the goals of care defined for the patient.

It is important to understand that hospice support is provided through scheduled visits rather than continuous 24-hour staffing. When a patient experiences a significant decline in functional ability, we work with you to clarify what additional support might be needed while continuing to provide our specialized clinical and comfort-focused care within the home setting.

Coverage and Evolving Needs

The Medicare hospice benefit covers professional clinical visits, equipment and medications focused on symptom management for the terminal illness. As a patient's functional needs evolve, we ensure that the services provided continue to address the most pressing needs, though the benefit generally does not cover long-term room and board.

We help families navigate the financial aspects of care, including identifying what is covered and what may require family-arranged private-duty assistance. Because we are committed to transparency, we openly discuss any limitations to the hospice benefit early on so that you can plan effectively for your loved one’s care.

Eligibility is always determined by clinical evaluation and physician certification. We work hard to ensure the care plan is robust enough to meet the patient’s changing condition. If your loved one’s care needs reach a point that exceeds the scope of the hospice benefit, we will discuss those specific transitions with you.

Common questions about hospice in Northridge

How often do you revisit the care plan?

Our clinical team reviews the plan of care regularly during every scheduled visit and through formal reassessments. If you notice a change in your loved one's functional ability between visits, please call us so we can discuss the changes and determine if an earlier update to the plan is necessary.

Does hospice include 24-hour supervision if my loved one can no longer manage tasks alone?

No, hospice is a visit-based clinical service. We provide intermittent nursing, aide and support visits according to an individualized plan. If your loved one requires 24-hour supervision or personal care, you may need to arrange for private-duty assistance to complement the hospice support provided.

What happens if the current plan no longer meets my loved one's needs?

When needs change, we perform a clinical reassessment in coordination with the patient's physician. We discuss these findings with your family to adjust the frequency and type of support provided within our hospice benefit, ensuring our care remains appropriately focused on the patient's comfort and current clinical goals.
