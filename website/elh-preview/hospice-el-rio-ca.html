Hospice Benefit and Scope of Care

The Medicare hospice benefit is designed to provide comprehensive, visit-based care related to the terminal illness. This includes professional nursing, aide services and symptom management. It is vital to note that this is not 24-hour care; hospice services are intermittent and intended to support you rather than take over all caregiving responsibilities.

Room and board are generally not covered by the hospice benefit and there may be limited copayments for some services or medications. Our admissions team can provide a detailed overview of what is covered so there are no surprises as you begin. We focus exclusively on the care related to the terminal condition, working alongside your primary physicians.

If you have questions about equipment or other daily living needs, we can discuss how these fit into your overall plan. Our hospice services follow the assessed medical need and we ensure that all care is delivered with the goal of maximizing comfort and quality of life according to the individualized plan of care.

Common questions about hospice in El Rio

How do I ensure hospice clinicians can easily reach my home?

When you call to arrange care, please provide clear information regarding the location of your residence in El Rio. Our admissions team will verify that our visit-based model works for your specific location and discuss any logistical details needed to ensure reliable, timely support for your loved one.

Does hospice provide 24-hour, in-home help?

No. Our hospice care is provided through scheduled, intermittent visits from our clinical team. We provide expert support and symptom management, but we do not offer continuous, around-the-clock staffing. Families typically manage daily needs and supervision, supplemented by our focused hospice visits as outlined in the care plan.

Can I continue to use my current physician?

Yes. Our team works collaboratively with the patient's existing physician to manage the care plan. We respect the ongoing relationship you have with your doctor and ensure they are kept informed of the patient's status throughout the duration of our hospice support.
