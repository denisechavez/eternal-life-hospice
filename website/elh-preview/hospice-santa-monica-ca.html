Navigating Benefits and Expenses

The Medicare hospice benefit provides comprehensive support for terminal-related care, but it is important to understand that it generally does not cover all medical needs. For example, standard room and board costs are generally not included under the benefit, even if you are residing in an assisted living community.

You should also be aware that while we coordinate essential medications related to the hospice diagnosis, some expenses or non-hospice medications may be the responsibility of the family. We encourage you to discuss copayments and individual costs with us early so there are no surprises regarding your coverage.

Remember that hospice does not replace the primary care provided by families or private-duty staff. Our visits occur as scheduled based on the assessment; we do not provide round-the-clock staffing or transport services, so continue to plan for your ongoing support needs alongside the hospice benefit.

Common questions about hospice in Santa Monica

Can I continue seeing my specialist if I start hospice?

Yes, you can maintain relationships with your existing doctors. The hospice team will coordinate with them to help all care align with the comfort-focused goals of the hospice benefit. We work together to help ensure that medications and treatments remain appropriate for the patient's overall plan.

What happens if I need treatment that isn't for my hospice diagnosis?

Treatments unrelated to the terminal diagnosis are typically managed outside the hospice benefit. We will work with your primary physician to clarify which therapies are covered by hospice and which should continue through your regular insurance. Always consult your doctor before discontinuing any medication or treatment.

Are all medications covered once I start hospice?

Hospice covers medications and supplies that are specifically related to the management of your terminal diagnosis and comfort. Medications unrelated to this diagnosis or for other health issues are not covered by the hospice benefit and will continue to be billed through your regular prescription drug coverage.
