Establishing Your Care Structure

Beginning hospice involves a clinical evaluation to certify eligibility. Once established, we meet with your family to define the communication structure. This allows us to confirm who should be contacted for updates, ensuring that information flows efficiently to those who need it most while respecting the family's wishes.

Hospice election is about managing symptoms and improving comfort, not necessarily stopping all medical treatment. We ask that you speak with your treating physicians about which medications and treatments continue under the hospice benefit. Our team provides comfort-focused care and does not offer medical triage or changes to current prescriptions.

To begin, use our professional referral form. We are available to discuss your needs and answer questions about how we coordinate with household staff or family members to maintain a calm and supportive environment for your loved one.

Navigating Financial and Care Commitments

Medicare hospice benefits cover most care related to your terminal diagnosis, but room and board costs in residential settings are typically the family's responsibility. It is important to review your coverage, as some limited copayments may apply for covered outpatient drugs or inpatient respite.

Please remember that our care is visit-based and distinct from the supervision provided by private-duty or facility staff. We work in tandem with your existing resources to provide high-quality clinical support. We do not offer continuous staff, equipment transport or 24-hour supervision services.

We aim to be transparent about what our benefit provides. If you have questions about specific expenses or the scope of our clinical visits, our team is ready to provide the information you need to make informed decisions for your family's future.

Common questions about hospice in San Marino

Who can make decisions about my loved one's care?

We ask that families identify a primary decision-maker or power of attorney during the initial assessment. This helps our team know who to consult when clinical choices arise. If multiple family members are involved, we can establish a communication plan to keep everyone informed.

How does your team communicate with household staff?

We coordinate with existing facility or household staff to ensure our clinical visits complement the care already being provided. We respect the roles of caregivers already present and focus our work on hospice-specific symptom management and clinical support for your loved one.

What happens if our primary contact is unavailable?

We recommend identifying an alternate contact or proxy when establishing your care plan. This helps our team have a point of contact for important updates or clinical questions. Please update us promptly if your contact information or decision-making roles change during your care.
