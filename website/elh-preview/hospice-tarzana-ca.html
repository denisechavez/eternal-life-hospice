Coordinating Hospice Services in Tarzana Residential Homes

Providing hospice in a residential care home requires a collaborative approach between our clinical team and the facility staff. When you choose hospice, we begin by reviewing the individualized plan of care to ensure that daily routines and support systems align with the patient’s goals while respecting the home's operational guidelines.

Effective communication ensures that staff members are prepared for changes in the patient's condition or comfort needs. Our role is to offer clinical guidance and education, supporting the facility staff in their ongoing care of your loved one, as we focus on managing symptoms and enhancing overall quality of life.

Steps to Integrating Care in Tarzana Residences

The process begins with a clinical evaluation to determine hospice eligibility. We discuss the patient’s health history and current goals with the primary physician to establish a certified plan of care that addresses specific comfort needs.

Once clinical eligibility is established, we meet with the family and facility leadership to discuss how our visits will complement the existing care routine. This meeting ensures that everyone understands the responsibilities of our team and the staff on-site, fostering clear expectations for support.

Because hospice is a team-based benefit, we maintain open lines of communication for updates or changes in care. Families play a vital role in sharing observations, while we provide the professional oversight necessary to adjust the plan of care as the patient’s needs evolve.

Understanding Hospice Coverage and Care Scope

Hospice is a specialized benefit and not all services are included. While Medicare provides coverage for core hospice services related to the terminal diagnosis, routine room and board costs at a residential facility generally remain the family's responsibility.

Individualized plans of care are developed based on medical necessity. We offer guidance on Pain and Symptom Management, but we cannot offer continuous supervision or private-duty care. Our staff coordinates with existing clinicians and we encourage you to discuss any existing treatments with your physician.

We help families navigate the How to Choose a Hospice process to ensure alignment. Copayments may apply for covered outpatient drugs or inpatient respite. Please reach out to our team to verify specific details regarding your loved one's benefit coverage.

Common questions about hospice in Tarzana

Will the hospice team be at the residential home 24/7?

No. Hospice provides scheduled, intermittent clinical visits based on assessed needs. We are not a substitute for the 24/7 supervision or daily living assistance provided by the residential facility staff. Our focus is on clinical symptom management and providing support for the family and primary caregivers.

How does the facility staff know what to do?

We coordinate directly with the facility staff to provide a clear, individualized plan of care. We offer education regarding the patient's comfort and symptom management so that facility staff can carry out their duties in alignment with the hospice goals we have established together.

What if the facility staff is not trained in hospice care?

Our hospice team provides guidance and education to facility staff to help them understand the patient's specific comfort needs. While we assist in explaining the hospice plan, we rely on the facility staff to maintain their standard duties while we support the specific end-of-life care goals.
