Understanding Costs and Benefits

The hospice benefit is designed to support the patient and family, but it generally does not cover every expense. For instance, room and board costs associated with a private home are generally the responsibility of the family, as the benefit is focused on medical support.

There may be limited copayments involved depending on the specific medications or services required. We encourage families to review their financial circumstances and ask questions about which costs are included under the benefit versus those that are private responsibilities.

Our goal is to provide transparency. Please reach out to our team to discuss the specific aspects of your coverage and how they apply to the care we provide in the Woodland Hills area.

Preparing Your Home

Before the transition, identify a main point of contact within your family who will serve as the primary liaison with our clinical staff. This helps streamline communication and ensures that updates regarding the patient condition reach us promptly.

Create a space that allows for easy access to the patient bed and any medical equipment. Removing clutter and ensuring adequate lighting are simple yet effective ways to enhance the safety and comfort of the environment for your loved one.

We encourage you to use our referral-form if you have specific questions about our clinical arrival procedures. Knowing the access layout of your home ahead of time allows us to plan our visits more effectively.

Common questions about hospice in Woodland Hills

How do we coordinate equipment when moving home?

Our team works with you to evaluate your home space. We discuss what current equipment is available and what adjustments or additions are necessary. We ensure that the transition plan aligns with the patient clinical needs while keeping your home environment safe and functional for care.

Does hospice cover assisted living or home rent?

The Medicare hospice benefit generally does not cover routine room and board expenses for assisted living or private homes. The benefit primarily covers services related to the terminal illness, such as clinical nursing visits, medications and support for symptoms. We can help you understand these coverage distinctions.

What medical records are required for a transition?

To ensure a smooth transition, we require a comprehensive list of all current medications, a record of the patient diagnoses and contact details for the treating physicians. Please use our professional referral form to provide these details so we can effectively coordinate the care plan.
