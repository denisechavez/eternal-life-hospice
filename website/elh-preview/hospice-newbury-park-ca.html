Common questions about hospice in Newbury Park

Does Eternal Life Hospice serve Newbury Park?

Yes. We provide Medicare-certified, ACHC-accredited hospice care to families throughout Newbury Park — at home, in assisted living, or in a skilled nursing facility. Call 805.953.7273 to speak with our team.

What parts of Newbury Park do you serve?

We serve all of Newbury Park, including Dos Vientos Ranch, Casa Conejo, Ventu Park, Rancho Conejo, and Old Town Newbury Park, along with neighboring Thousand Oaks, Westlake Village, Camarillo, and the Wildwood area.

Is hospice covered by Medicare?

Yes. Medicare Part A covers hospice care in full when a physician certifies a life expectancy of six months or less — including nursing visits, comfort medications, medical equipment, aide services, social work, chaplain support, and volunteer visits, at no cost to the patient.

How quickly can care begin in Newbury Park?

We offer same-day admissions. Once a physician order is in place, our team can often begin care the same day. Call 805.953.7273.

What makes Eternal Life Hospice different?

Eternal Life Hospice is independent, with a physician-led clinical model, a nurse on call 24/7, and integrative comfort therapies — including music, massage, reiki, aromatherapy, and original sound bath journeys — included in every care plan at no extra cost.
