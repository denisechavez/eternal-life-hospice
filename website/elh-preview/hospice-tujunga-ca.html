Understanding Service Delivery and Limitations

Hospice is a team-based, intermittent service. It is not designed to replace private-duty caregivers or to provide 24-hour supervision. Our visits are scheduled to manage symptoms and support your family, but they are distinct from the continuous care you might receive in a facility or through private help.

Coverage under Medicare is based on clinical certification of need. We assist families in understanding which aspects of care are covered, including medications related to the hospice diagnosis, while explaining that room and board or non-related medical expenses are typically not covered by the hospice benefit.

Because care is visit-based, we do not promise specific arrival times or immediate dispatch. We ask that families maintain open lines of communication regarding any changes in the patient's health, as our care plan is built on the clinical input of the treating physician and our ongoing team assessments.

Common questions about hospice in Tujunga

How do you handle visits in my canyon neighborhood?

Our team reviews the access to your specific address as part of our initial intake process. We prioritize the safety of our staff and the reliability of our services, so we ask families to be open about any local road or access limitations that might impact our visit schedule.

Does hospice provide 24-hour care at home?

Hospice is a visit-based service, not a 24-hour, in-home personal care service. While we provide specialized support for symptom management and comfort, we do not offer continuous, around-the-clock supervision. Families should consider whether supplemental private care is needed to assist with daily living tasks.

What if I need help moving to a facility later?

We focus on providing hospice support in your current setting. If your needs change and you move to a facility, we will work with you to reassess the care plan. We do not provide physical moving services or equipment transport, but we can help coordinate care across different living environments.
