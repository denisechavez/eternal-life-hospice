Clinical Scope and Benefit Details

Hospice care is a specific Medicare benefit designed to support patients at home. It is important to note that room and board costs are generally not covered for those residing at home or in standard assisted living facilities. While many medications related to the terminal diagnosis may be covered, coverage limitations apply.

Election of the hospice benefit does not automatically mean all existing medical treatments must stop. We will help you review which treatments remain appropriate for the patient's comfort. Please consult with your primary doctor regarding current prescriptions or ongoing medical procedures to ensure they align with the hospice plan.

We do not promise continuous staffing or 24-hour supervision. Our visits are scheduled according to clinical need. For urgent medical concerns, please consult your primary physician or reach out to our team via phone rather than expecting immediate triage through our website.

Common questions about hospice in Signal Hill

Why is it necessary to verify my address before scheduling an evaluation?

Because hospice care relies on frequent, scheduled visits from our clinical team, we must verify that your specific address falls within our current service capacity. This helps us evaluate the logistics of travel throughout the South Bay region.

Does choosing hospice mean I can no longer see my personal doctor?

No. Your primary physician remains an essential part of your care team. We work closely with them to certify hospice eligibility and ensure that the care plan we design is consistent with your medical history and goals, focusing on maximizing comfort in your home environment.

Are all medications and supplies covered by the hospice benefit?

Not necessarily. The hospice benefit covers medications and supplies directly related to the terminal diagnosis, provided they are part of the individualized plan of care. Other expenses or non-hospice-related medications may remain the responsibility of the family or other insurance plans. We will help you clarify these details.
