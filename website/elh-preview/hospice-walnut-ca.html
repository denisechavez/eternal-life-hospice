Asking for Care Plan Clarity

A care plan is a living document that changes based on the patient's needs. We encourage you to ask for an explanation of the plan in simple terms. Understanding why certain treatments are included and how we manage symptoms helps everyone feel more confident. There is no such thing as a bad question when it comes to the comfort of your loved one.

Hospice requires physician certification and clinical evaluation. We do not base services on age or diagnosis alone, but rather on the current medical assessment. During the first visit, our team identifies the patient's primary needs and discusses how we can best provide comfort. We encourage families to voice their concerns immediately so we can refine the approach.

Clear communication involves regular updates between the family and our nursing staff. When you understand the care plan, you are better equipped to report changes in symptoms to our team. We view care as a partnership and your input is essential for ensuring the plan remains effective and aligned with the patient's goals.

Understanding What Hospice Covers

The hospice benefit covers services related to the terminal illness, including medications, nursing visits and medical equipment as determined by the plan. Routine room and board are generally not covered and copayments may apply for some services. We provide a breakdown of these details to ensure there are no surprises during care.

Existing treatments are evaluated to see if they fit within the hospice framework. Some treatments may continue, while others might be modified to prioritize comfort. Please do not change any medication or care routine without consulting with your treating clinician or our hospice team. We are dedicated to safe and effective management.

Hospice is visit-based, which means we do not provide continuous care or private-duty staffing. We support the patient and the family by providing professional guidance and symptom relief. If your needs exceed what hospice provides, we will discuss how to arrange for additional help or navigate the appropriate resources for long-term support.

Common questions about hospice in Walnut

How can I get a copy of the care plan?

You can request a summary of the current care plan from your primary hospice nurse at any time. We believe families should be fully informed, so we will provide an explanation that clearly outlines the services provided and the goals for the patient's comfort and symptom management.

What if I do not understand why a medication was changed?

If you are confused about a medication change, please contact our hospice team immediately. We will explain the clinical reasoning behind the change and how it relates to the patient's comfort. Never hesitate to ask for clarity; understanding the purpose of each medication is vital to the care plan.

Does the care plan include 24-hour nursing supervision?

No, hospice provides intermittent visit-based care, not 24-hour continuous supervision. The care plan includes scheduled visits from our team, but the patient's primary caregiver is responsible for daily needs outside those visits. We will explain the level of support included so you can plan appropriately for your family's needs.
