Reconfirming Care During Transitions

When a patient moves, the first step is to inform our team immediately so we can update the plan of care. Each environment, whether a home or a facility, has distinct operational procedures that our clinical staff must understand to provide safe and effective visits to the patient.

We encourage families to contact us as soon as a transition is planned. This allows us to coordinate with the new residential staff, clarify the hospice role and ensure that all necessary clinical information is communicated effectively. It is essential that we reconfirm access and care expectations for the new location.

Financial and Care Implications of Moves

Changing residential settings can influence how the hospice benefit is applied. While hospice remains a consistent support system, the costs associated with your housing—such as room and board at a facility—are typically not covered by the hospice benefit and will continue to be the responsibility of the family.

We provide clear information on how these transitions affect care coverage, noting that certain copayments may still apply. It is important to distinguish between the clinical hospice services we provide and the daily living expenses associated with different residential environments, as these remain separate from our care.

We do not promise specific staffing ratios or coverage of all non-hospice expenses. Instead, we work to ensure that our hospice services are clearly defined and that you understand how our interdisciplinary team integrates into your chosen setting to support the patient's comfort and quality of life.

Common questions about hospice in Valencia

What happens to the hospice plan if we move?

When you move, the hospice plan must be re-evaluated to reflect the new environment. We update our care plan to ensure it meets the requirements of the new residence and continues to provide the support the patient needs while maintaining clinical alignment with the treating physician's goals.

Are all residential facilities covered by hospice?

Hospice services can be provided in many residential settings, but each facility has its own rules. We work with you to coordinate with the facility staff, but we cannot guarantee that every facility will allow our hospice team to operate or that their specific policies will change for our services.

Do I need to notify my insurance if I move?

While the hospice benefit is generally portable, any move should be reported to us immediately so we can manage the transition of care. We will handle the coordination with the hospice side of your benefits, but you should also discuss any residential cost changes with the facility management directly.
