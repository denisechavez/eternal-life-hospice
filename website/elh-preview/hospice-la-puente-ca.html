Beginning Your Care Journey

Beginning hospice involves a clinical review and collaboration with your doctor to certify the need for our care. We prioritize transparency during this initial phase, ensuring your family understands the focus of our visits and how communication will be handled. This alignment is vital for a smooth transition into our support services.

We recognize that family roles shift during a health transition. Our team works with you to establish clear protocols for reporting visit outcomes, ensuring that those at work or away from home receive the necessary information. We do not provide constant staffing, but we do provide reliable, visit-based clinical and emotional support.

During the admission process, we discuss your goals for comfort and symptom management. This plan is individualized and reviewed regularly as your needs change. We encourage open questions regarding the care plan to ensure our visits align with the priorities you have set for your family and your loved one.

Coverage, Benefits and Expectations

The Medicare hospice benefit provides coverage for services related to the terminal illness, though some limitations apply to medications and other expenses. Routine room and board are not covered under this benefit. It is helpful to clarify these financial considerations early to avoid unexpected surprises, as we prioritize honesty in every conversation.

Our care is centered on visits from our interdisciplinary team. We do not provide or guarantee continuous home monitoring, medical triage or private-duty care. We help bridge the gap for families managing care at home, but families should note that these services are separate from other private-duty or facility-based arrangements they may already have.

If you have questions regarding your specific insurance or the costs associated with hospice, our team is available to assist. We focus on providing high-quality support that is covered by your benefit, ensuring you understand how to utilize these resources effectively during this time of adjustment for your family.

Common questions about hospice in La Puente

How do you handle updates when the primary family caregiver is at work?

We establish a clear communication plan at the start of care. We can provide updates via phone or secure methods as requested, ensuring that important clinical information is shared with the appropriate family contact even when you are unavailable during the workday.

Is hospice care 24 hours a day?

Hospice is a visit-based service, not a 24-hour monitoring or staffing service. Our team visits according to an individualized schedule to provide comfort and support. If additional supervision is required, families may need to arrange for private-duty assistance or facility-based care.

What do I need to prepare before my first visit?

Prepare a list of your loved one's current medications and any specific symptoms that are causing the most discomfort. Having a clear idea of your communication preferences and the primary contact's schedule also helps us align our care with your family's needs from the very first visit.
