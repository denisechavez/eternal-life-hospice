Planning for Your Equipment Needs

When transitioning care, start by identifying the specific equipment currently in use, such as hospital beds, oxygen or mobility aids. Having a complete inventory helps us coordinate with your medical team to ensure that equipment needs are addressed as part of the overarching hospice plan.

Consider the logistical requirements of moving these items between residences. Does the new location have adequate space and power configurations? Discussing these practicalities early in the process allows for better preparation and reduces stress when the time for a transition arrives.

Medicare Benefit and Care Planning

The Medicare hospice benefit focuses on clinical care related to the terminal diagnosis. It generally does not cover long-term room and board, nor does it guarantee the movement or storage of personal medical equipment between homes. These factors should be considered as you plan your transitions.

Coverage includes essential items and medications that are part of the individualized plan of care. However, there may be limited copayments for certain services. We encourage families to review their benefits carefully and discuss any financial questions with our team to avoid misunderstandings during your care journey.

Our goal is to provide transparency so you can focus on the patient. We assist by coordinating the clinical aspects of your care and advising on how the hospice benefit aligns with your specific goals and needs during this transition in the Conejo Valley.

Common questions about hospice in Agoura Hills

How do you coordinate equipment when moving homes?

We focus on the clinical transfer of care. While we help organize the medical plan, families are generally responsible for the physical logistics of moving equipment. We advise you to contact us as soon as you anticipate a move so we can ensure the care plan is updated for the new address.

Does hospice provide 24/7 bedside sitters?

Hospice provides intermittent nursing and support visits based on clinical need. We do not provide continuous, 24/7 sitting or private-duty care. Families often arrange for additional support through private agencies if constant supervision is required. We are happy to discuss how our visits supplement that care.

Will changing homes interrupt the hospice benefit?

Moving can impact service delivery, especially if you move to a new area outside our immediate service zone. Please inform us immediately if you plan to move so we can verify coverage at the new address and ensure the transition of clinical documentation is as effective as possible.
