Coordinating Hospice with Your Encino Facility

Beginning hospice care in a facility involves a clinical assessment to ensure the patient meets eligibility requirements and that our visit-based model is appropriate. We begin by meeting with the facility administrator and the patient’s physician to establish a care plan that respects the facility's existing policies while prioritizing the patient's comfort and dignity.

A common question is whether the resident must stop receiving facility services. The answer is no; our care is an enhancement. We work with the facility team to identify which tasks they will continue to perform and which specific hospice interventions our nurses and aides will manage. This clarity helps prevent service gaps and ensures the resident receives comprehensive support.

We encourage families to facilitate a joint meeting between our team and the facility nursing staff. By opening this dialogue, we can create a plan that addresses symptoms effectively and identifies who to contact for specific needs. Our primary goal is to advocate for the patient's preferences while maintaining a calm, cooperative environment within their home facility.

Understanding Hospice Benefit and Facility Care

The Medicare hospice benefit covers specialized care related to the terminal diagnosis, but it does not generally cover the cost of room and board charged by an assisted living or board and care facility. These expenses remain the responsibility of the family or the resident. It is essential to review your specific facility contract to distinguish between room/board and clinical services.

When electing the hospice benefit, certain medications or therapies may be covered if they are directly related to the life-limiting condition. We help coordinate with the facility to ensure that our clinical team is informed of all treatments and that any new medications related to the hospice diagnosis are managed under our plan. We do not alter existing facility-managed care plans.

If a resident requires a higher level of care, such as short-term inpatient respite to manage acute pain, we may assist in coordinating that transition according to benefit guidelines. Our team will explain the process and requirements, ensuring families have the information necessary to make informed decisions about coverage and the next steps for their loved one.

Common questions about hospice in Encino

Does hospice replace the staff at my loved one's facility?

No, hospice is an additional service. Facility staff continue to provide daily living assistance, meals and supervision. Our hospice team provides specialized clinical support, such as pain and symptom management, while working in coordination with the facility staff to ensure the patient's care is consistent and well-managed.

Will the facility bill change after starting hospice?

Hospice is a separate benefit. The facility will continue to charge for room and board, which hospice does not cover. If you have questions about billing, please contact the facility administration regarding your contract and our hospice billing department for inquiries about clinical services covered by the Medicare hospice benefit.

Can my loved one still receive therapy from the facility?

Yes, if the facility provides therapies, they may continue. We review all current treatments and therapies to ensure they align with the hospice plan of care. We encourage open communication between the hospice team, the facility staff and your family to determine which therapies are appropriate under the hospice election.
