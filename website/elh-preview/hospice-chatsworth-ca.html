The Process of Equipment Assessment

When we begin care, an assessment is conducted to determine what equipment might support the patient's comfort. This is a clinical decision based on the patient's specific needs, such as mobility limitations or the need for a specialized bed. We look at the home layout to see where items could be placed, but this is an initial evaluation, not a promise that every piece of gear will be a perfect fit.

Families are often concerned about how much space equipment will take up. We emphasize that equipment is meant to serve the patient's clinical comfort, not to change the fundamental nature of the home. Our team will discuss which items are most important and how they are typically used in a home setting, ensuring that you have the information needed to make informed decisions for your loved one.

Eligibility for these tools is linked to the patient's individualized plan and must be certified as part of the hospice care process. Equipment Logistics and Limitations

It is important to understand that equipment coverage through the Medicare hospice benefit is specific to items that are necessary for the palliation of the terminal diagnosis. Not all mobility aids or home modifications are covered and we cannot promise that every piece of suggested equipment will be provided at zero expense. There may be limitations on what is considered clinically necessary.

Our hospice team does not provide ongoing equipment movement or permanent home modifications. We suggest that you check with your insurance or private providers if you need items that fall outside of the hospice-covered benefit. Our goal is to ensure you understand the boundaries of our care so you can prepare accordingly.

If you have questions about whether a specific item might be covered, please consult with our clinical team. We provide guidance based on the hospice plan, but we encourage you to maintain communication with your treating physician regarding the overall medical necessity and any non-hospice expenses that may arise.

Common questions about hospice in Chatsworth

Can you guarantee that a hospital bed will fit in my loved one's room?

No. We can perform an assessment to check the space, but we cannot guarantee that any specific equipment will fit or be suitable for your home's layout. We prioritize patient safety and comfort, but the final decision regarding space and placement rests with the family.

Does hospice provide permanent structural changes to the home?

No. We do not provide or cover home modifications such as ramps, grab bars or structural room changes. Our assessment is focused on identifying clinical equipment that helps manage symptoms, not on altering the physical structure of your residence.

Who is responsible for moving equipment if we need to relocate it?

Families are responsible for any non-clinical moving of equipment within the home. Our team's role is to assess the clinical necessity and placement of equipment related to the hospice plan, but we do not provide ongoing maintenance, cleaning or relocation services for items once they are placed.
