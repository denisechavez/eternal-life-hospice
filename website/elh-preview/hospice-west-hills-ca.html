Common questions about hospice in West Hills

Does Eternal Life Hospice serve West Hills?

Yes. We provide Medicare-certified, ACHC-accredited hospice care to families throughout West Hills — at home, in assisted living, or in a skilled nursing facility. Call 805.953.7273 to speak with our team.

What parts of West Hills do you serve?

We serve all of West Hills, including Bell Canyon, Hillcrest Knolls, and the Chatsworth border, along with neighboring Woodland Hills, Canoga Park, Chatsworth, and Calabasas.

Is hospice covered by Medicare?

Yes. Medicare Part A covers hospice care in full when a physician certifies a life expectancy of six months or less — including nursing visits, comfort medications, medical equipment, aide services, social work, chaplain support, and volunteer visits, at no cost to the patient.

How quickly can care begin in West Hills?

We offer same-day admissions when clinically appropriate. Once a physician order is in place, our team can often begin care the same day. Call 805.953.7273.

What makes Eternal Life Hospice different?

Eternal Life Hospice is an independent agency serving the western San Fernando Valley, with a physician-supported clinical model, a nurse on call at every hour, and integrative comfort therapies — including music therapy, therapeutic massage, and aromatherapy — available when appropriate, at no additional expense. Our team coordinates with local medical offices and visits patients from Fallbrook Avenue to Bell Canyon.
