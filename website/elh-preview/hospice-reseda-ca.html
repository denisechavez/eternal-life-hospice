Coverage and Clinical Oversight

While the Medicare hospice benefit provides comprehensive coverage for items related to the terminal diagnosis, it is important to understand what falls under this umbrella. We assist you in identifying covered symptoms and supplies, though coverage is not a blanket guarantee for all household expenses or non-hospice related items.

Professional clinical oversight ensures that our documentation process aligns with regulatory requirements, providing you with peace of mind that care is being managed safely. If you have questions about specific medications or symptoms, please direct these to our clinical team rather than adjusting treatments on your own.

We strive to provide transparency regarding what is covered and what may require family coordination. By discussing your specific needs during the initial assessment and ongoing visits, we can help clarify the limits of the hospice benefit and ensure you have the information needed to make informed choices.

Common questions about hospice in Reseda

Why is it important for me to document symptoms at home?

Documentation provides our clinicians with a clear timeline of changes in the patient's condition. This data helps us make informed decisions about your care plan, supports our efforts to provide effective interventions and allows us to provide more targeted support based on your actual experiences between visits.

What should I do if I have a question about a new symptom?

Always record the symptom and the time it occurred, then contact our clinical team at 805.953.7273. Please do not attempt to change medications or treatments without speaking to us or the patient's physician first. We will use your documentation to guide our assessment and determine the best course of action.

Does my documentation help with Medicare requirements?

Yes, thorough documentation is essential for demonstrating the clinical need required by the Medicare hospice benefit. It allows us to verify that the patient meets eligibility criteria and helps us justify the continued intensity of services as the patient’s health needs evolve over time.
