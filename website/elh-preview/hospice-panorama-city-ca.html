Understanding Care Scope and Benefits

It is important to understand that hospice provides visit-based support, which is distinct from the 24-hour staffing or private-duty care provided by a board-and-care home. Room and board costs at a residential facility are generally not covered by the medicare hospice benefit.

While the benefit may cover medications, supplies and equipment directly related to the terminal diagnosis, other expenses or facility fees typically remain the responsibility of the family. We recommend reviewing your specific insurance coverage and facility contract to understand potential costs.

Eligibility for our program is based on a clinical assessment, not solely on a specific diagnosis or age. Services are provided according to an individualized plan of care that addresses the patient's immediate comfort and clinical requirements.

Common questions about hospice in Panorama City

Does hospice replace the staff at the board-and-care home?

No. Hospice is a specialized, visit-based service that focuses on comfort and symptom management. It works alongside the facility's existing staff who provide daily assistance with meals, bathing and supervision. We coordinate to ensure care plans remain aligned and supportive of the resident's comfort.

Who pays for the room and board at the facility?

Routine room and board costs are typically not covered by the medicare hospice benefit. These expenses generally remain the responsibility of the family or the patient's existing payment arrangements with the facility. We can help you understand what clinical services the hospice benefit does cover.

Can my loved one keep their current doctor?

Yes. We encourage patients to maintain a relationship with their primary physician. Our hospice team coordinates with your loved one's doctor to ensure that any comfort-focused clinical plans remain consistent with their overall health history and medical status. Please consult your physician regarding any changes in treatment.
