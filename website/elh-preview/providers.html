A compliance-led referral partner

Referring physicians, discharge planners and case managers carry real responsibility for every hand-off. Eternal Life Hospice is built to make that hand-off clean: a nationally accredited, Medicare-certified agency that confirms appropriateness, documents thoroughly and keeps you informed.

We pair the regulatory discipline you need with the human, integrative care your patients deserve — but the rigor comes first, because that is what protects you and your patient.

Credentials you can stand behind

Every referral to Eternal Life Hospice is backed by current, verifiable credentials — the same standards your surveyors and your patients expect.

Medicare-Certified

Certified by the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services, so the hospice benefit is delivered to federal standards — with no deductibles or copays for eligible patients.

CDPH-Licensed

Licensed by the California Department of Public Health and accountable to state oversight, inspection and reporting requirements.

ACHC-Accredited

Accredited by the Accreditation Commission for Health Care — an independent, nationally recognized seal of clinical and operational quality.

Eligibility, confirmed correctly

A patient may be appropriate for the Medicare hospice benefit when a physician certifies a terminal prognosis of six months or less if the illness runs its normal course, and the goals of care shift from cure to comfort. We help you make that determination with confidence.

We confirm appropriateness before admission. Our clinical team reviews the diagnosis and prognosis with you so the referral is sound and well-documented — helping reduce the risk of an inappropriate or delayed admission.

Documentation and face-to-face, handled

The paperwork that protects you is our responsibility too. We manage the regulatory record end to end and return what you need on time.

Certification of Terminal Illness

Accurate, timely certification and recertification handled in coordination with the attending and hospice physician.

Face-to-Face encounters

Required F2F encounters completed within regulatory windows so eligibility stays compliant through every benefit period.

Records back to you

HIPAA-secure exchange
