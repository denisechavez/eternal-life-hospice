Clarifying Hospice Benefits and Support

Medicare hospice provides essential support for terminal conditions, though it does not typically cover the cost of room and board in long-term care settings. Families should also be aware that limited copayments may apply for covered outpatient drugs or inpatient respite. We provide full transparency about what our benefit includes.

It is important to understand that hospice visits are scheduled based on the patient's needs and are not a replacement for 24-hour facility staff or personal caregivers. We do not provide continuous bedside monitoring, equipment movement or transport services. We focus on specialized hospice clinical care.

We are happy to answer your questions about costs and coverage. Contact us to learn more about how our visit-based model supports your loved one while complementing the care already provided by your chosen residence staff.

Common questions about hospice in San Pedro

Can you provide care in any residential setting?

We provide care in many settings, including private homes and residential facilities. However, because our care is visit-based, we must confirm that we can access your specific location. Please contact us with your address so we can determine if we can serve your loved one's current residence.

What is the difference between your care and facility staff?

Our team focuses on specialized hospice clinical support and symptom management, while facility staff typically provide the 24-hour daily living assistance. We work in tandem with the facility to help our care plan complement the support your loved one already receives in their environment.

How does hospice impact current medication or treatment?

Hospice election does not mean all medical treatment stops. We focus on comfort-related care. We ask that you speak with your treating physician to determine which treatments remain active under the hospice benefit and we do not provide triage or change your existing medication prescriptions.
