Careers at Eternal Life Hospice

If caring well is what you do best, we would like to meet you. We are building a team that brings clinical confidence and human presence to families in their most tender moments — close to the communities you call home.

Work that matters, close to home

Eternal Life Hospice is an independent, Medicare-certified and ACHC-accredited agency growing across Ventura County and Los Angeles County. We are integrative by design, with whole-person integrative therapies available when appropriate, at no additional expense to families.

We believe the people who give care deserve to be cared for too. That means manageable caseloads, a team that answers when you call, and the freedom to practice the way good clinicians know is right — with time, attention and respect for the family in the room.

Who we welcome

We are hiring across clinical and supporting roles. A few of the people we hope to meet:

Registered nurses & LVNs

Case managers and visit nurses who lead with clinical judgment and a calm, steady presence at the bedside.

Certified home health aides

The hands-on comfort and dignity of daily care, delivered with patience and warmth.

Social workers

Guiding families through decisions, resources and the emotional weight of the journey.

Chaplains & spiritual care

Honoring every belief and tradition, offering presence and meaning at the end of life.

Bereavement counselors

Walking with families through the months that follow a loss, with skill and compassion.

Integrative therapists

Music, massage, sound bath and more — bringing whole-person comfort into every plan of care.

Outreach & community liaisons

Connecting families and referral partners with the care they need, when they need it.

Office & administrative

The behind-the-scenes coordination that keeps compassionate care running smoothly.

The Eternal Standard. Every role here is held to the same promise we make families: clinical confidence, guided presence, whole-person comfort and compliance-led care. If that is how you want to practice, you will be at home with us.

How to join us

The process is personal and straightforward. We want to understand your strengths and find the place where you will do your best work.

Reach out

Send a note or call. Tell us about your background, your license or specialty and the role that draws you.

A conversation

We talk through your experience, what you are looking for and how you like to work.

Credentials & onboarding

We verify licensure and clearances and prepare you fully for the team you are joining.

Welcome to the team

You start with the support, training and caseload that let you give care the way it should be given.

Where you’ll be based

Find us at Suite 325B

When you join Eternal Life Hospice, this is the door you’ll walk through. We are a real, independent agency with a local home office — close to the Ventura and Los Angeles County communities we serve.
