Coordinating Hospice With Your Facility

When a patient resides in a facility, the hospice team functions as a supplemental layer of care. We conduct clinical assessments and provide symptom management, while the facility staff continues to provide ongoing daily assistance. Establishing a clear communication channel between our team, the facility staff and your family is a priority during the initial assessment.

We encourage families to talk with their facility administration before starting hospice services. Understanding how our team fits into their existing protocols—such as how medications are managed or how updates are shared—is crucial. Hospice care is not intended to bypass facility rules or replace the primary responsibilities of the facility team, but rather to enhance the level of care focused on the patient's quality of life.

Eligibility for hospice requires a physician's certification that the patient has a life-limiting condition. Once admitted, we create an individualized plan that reflects both the hospice goals and the environment of the facility. Coverage and Facility Considerations

It is important to understand that Medicare hospice benefits generally do not cover the room and board expenses charged by your facility. These are typically independent costs paid by the family or other private arrangements. Our hospice services focus specifically on the clinical needs related to the terminal diagnosis.

We coordinate closely regarding medications and supplies related to the hospice diagnosis, but we do not cover all medical expenses or treatments unrelated to the hospice plan. Families should continue to communicate with the facility about non-hospice costs and care requirements. Any copayments or coverage limitations will be discussed during the intake process.

If you have specific concerns about how the hospice benefit interacts with your current facility arrangements, we are available to answer your questions. Our team provides professional guidance to ensure that all parties understand their respective roles in supporting the patient's comfort and dignity throughout their time in hospice.

Common questions about hospice in Cerritos

Will the hospice team replace the care my loved one receives from the facility staff?

No. Our team provides specialized clinical support focused on hospice goals, but we do not replace the daily custodial care, meals or assistance provided by the facility. We work collaboratively with the facility team to ensure your loved one's care plan remains coordinated and effective.

How does hospice handle medications if the facility manages them?

We coordinate with the facility staff regarding medications related to the hospice diagnosis. However, the facility's internal policies for medication administration remain in place. We do not personally dispense or supervise the facility staff's administration process, nor do we provide medical triage for non-hospice medications.

Can I request specific hours for hospice visits at the facility?

Visits are scheduled based on the patient's assessed clinical needs and the nurse's clinical schedule. While we strive to be mindful of facility routines, visits are not guaranteed for specific times of the day or week. We focus on ensuring the patient receives the appropriate clinical oversight as planned.
