Hospice Benefits and Practical Realities

The Medicare hospice benefit provides a structured approach to terminal care, but it is important to maintain realistic expectations. Routine room and board costs are generally not covered and the benefit is not intended to replace the primary care or supervision provided by family members, facility staff or private-duty providers.

Coverage includes medications and supplies that are directly related to the hospice plan of care. Other expenses, including those for conditions unrelated to the terminal diagnosis, are not covered under this benefit. We are happy to discuss these financial realities with you to ensure your family can plan accordingly.

Please understand that we cannot promise 24-hour staffing or immediate visit availability. Our visits are scheduled based on the patient's individual needs. We are here to support your family's journey with compassion and practical, evidence-based care within our service capacity in the Santa Clara River Valley.

Common questions about hospice in Santa Paula

How can I organize records if my family and doctors are in different cities?

Start by requesting copies of recent clinical notes from each of the patient's specialists. Using our professional referral form, you can provide us with these contact details and summaries. Our team will then reach out to these providers to ensure all pertinent health information is integrated into your care plan.

Does your team help manage care if we use different facilities?

Yes, we coordinate with the staff at residential care homes and assisted living facilities where our patients reside. We work alongside the facility's existing staff to ensure our hospice plan complements their routine care, focusing specifically on comfort, symptom management and supporting the patient's end-of-life goals.

Can I get help understanding what is and isn't covered by hospice?

Absolutely. We encourage families to sit down with us to review the Medicare hospice benefit. We can clarify which medications and services are covered for the terminal diagnosis and help you identify other resources for non-hospice needs. Transparency is key to a comfortable experience.
