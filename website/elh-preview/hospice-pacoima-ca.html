Clinical Evaluation and Plan of Care

Eligibility for hospice relies on physician certification and a clinical assessment to ensure our services meet the patient's specific health goals. We provide specialized support that is distinct from the care you might receive from private-duty agencies or facility staff.

While many patients wonder about coverage for medications or supplies, please note that the Medicare hospice benefit has specific guidelines. Not every expense is covered and we will help you clarify which items fall under the hospice plan versus personal costs during our initial intake.

Common questions about hospice in Pacoima

What if I cannot physically assist with all patient care tasks?

Our team evaluates your current environment to identify which duties can be supported by our staff and which require additional outside resources. We focus on teaching caregivers practical skills and assisting where clinical expertise is necessary, ensuring a safe and balanced experience for both you and your loved one.

Do you provide round-the-clock staffing in the home?

Hospice care is visit-based rather than continuous. We provide scheduled clinical visits based on the patient's assessed need and the established plan of care. We work closely with you to teach caregiving techniques, but we are not a substitute for 24-hour private-duty home care or nursing home staffing.

Will your staff handle all equipment and medication needs?

While we provide support for symptoms related to the terminal diagnosis, we do not cover all household expenses or manage non-hospice treatments. We will discuss which medications and supplies are covered under your benefit and provide guidance on how to manage remaining household or personal care needs effectively.
