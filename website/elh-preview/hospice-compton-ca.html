Clarifying Hospice Benefits and Responsibilities

The Medicare Hospice Benefit provides a framework for comfort-focused care, including medication and supplies directly associated with the patient's diagnosis. It is important to clarify that this benefit generally does not cover room and board in a facility setting and some limited copayments may apply. We believe in being transparent about what is covered to help you make informed decisions.

While we provide specialized clinical support, hospice is not a substitute for 24-hour private-duty help. We coordinate closely with the patient's primary physician and the designated family contact to ensure that our care is well-integrated with other aspects of the patient's life. We encourage families to review the Medicare Hospice Benefit page for more detail.

Hospice is a voluntary election and the patient or their authorized representative can choose to stop or change services at any time. We are committed to helping you understand your rights and the details of your coverage so that you can navigate this experience with confidence and peace of mind.

Common questions about hospice in Compton

Why is a single primary update contact recommended?

Designating one primary contact ensures that critical information about the patient's care plan and condition is shared consistently and accurately. This helps avoid confusion and allows our clinical team to focus on providing high-quality care during our home visits.

Does a primary contact have the authority to change the care plan?

A primary contact is our main partner for communication and updates. While they are vital for relaying information, decisions regarding care plan changes often involve the patient, the physician and any legally authorized representative, ensuring all clinical decisions are properly made.

Can other family members still get updates?

The primary contact can share information with the wider family as they see fit. This approach keeps our communication focused and efficient, ensuring that the most important updates are communicated reliably to everyone while respecting the patient's privacy and our clinical staff's time.
