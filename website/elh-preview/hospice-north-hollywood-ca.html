Beginning Care with Site Awareness

Starting hospice care in a managed North Hollywood residence involves ensuring our clinicians have a clear path to the patient. During the admission process, we discuss the practical logistics of your building, including any entry requirements, call boxes or visitor registration policies that our staff must follow to provide care consistently.

It is important to remember that hospice is a visit-based service, not a 24-hour facility or private-duty service. Our staff visit according to an established schedule to evaluate the patient and provide support. For this reason, maintaining clear communication with your building’s management regarding visitor access is a helpful way to prepare for our upcoming visits.

By addressing building access early, we can minimize potential interruptions. If your loved one’s needs change, please contact us immediately so we can adjust our visit strategy to better support the patient and your family within your home.

Coverage and Care Planning

The Medicare hospice benefit provides coverage for services directly related to the terminal illness, including nursing visits, aide support and medical supplies. This benefit is intended for care in the home, though it does not cover the costs of housing, meals or standard facility fees associated with managed buildings.

We understand that living in a managed building sometimes raises questions about how hospice fits with existing services. Hospice is a distinct service that coordinates with your current team rather than replacing your personal home health or building staff. We are happy to clarify which services fall under the hospice benefit and which are separate.

Eligibility for the hospice benefit requires a physician’s certification and a clinical assessment. Once these criteria are met, we build a plan that focuses on symptom management and comfort. We are committed to transparency regarding coverage, including any copayments for medications, so please ask us if you have specific financial questions.

Common questions about hospice in North Hollywood

Will you handle the access arrangements with my building management?

We ask that families facilitate the primary communication with building management or security regarding visitor access. Once you have established the protocol, we will adhere to those requirements to ensure our clinical staff can enter the building smoothly for all scheduled visits.

Is hospice care the same as having a 24-hour caregiver?

No, hospice is a visit-based service, not a 24-hour private-duty or facility-based staffing service. We coordinate with the family and any existing caregivers to provide expert clinical support and symptom management, but we do not provide continuous, in-home supervision or dedicated staffing ratios.

What happens if my loved one needs care that requires a building lift or special equipment?

Hospice does not move, install or manage heavy facility equipment. We focus on providing comfort through nursing and aide visits. If special equipment is required for the patient’s mobility, please consult your physician and the building management team regarding the appropriate logistics.
