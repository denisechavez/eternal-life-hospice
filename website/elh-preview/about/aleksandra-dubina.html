Leadership at Eternal Life Hospice

An Agency Built on Personal Purpose

Aleksandra Dubina founded Eternal Life Hospice from a place most administrators never reach: personal knowledge of what it means to sit at a loved one's side during the final chapter of their life.

Eternal Life Hospice is an independent, Medicare-certified and ACHC-accredited agency serving families across Ventura County and Los Angeles County. As founder and chief executive, Aleksandra sets the clinical, operational and ethical standard for every member of the care team — and remains a hands-on presence who knows every patient by name.

Why Hospice Became Personal

Two Experiences That Shaped a Career

Her grandmother passed on hospice. That experience — the profound relief of attentive, dignified end-of-life care — became the reason she entered this field, and the standard she sets for every member of her team.

As a young child, Aleksandra underwent open-heart surgery, brought to the United States for advanced care. Having once been the patient — wholly dependent on the skill and compassion of others — she leads with a rare understanding of what it means to be on the receiving end of care.

Healthcare & Hospice Experience

More Than Two Decades in Care

With more than two decades in healthcare — eleven of those years in hospice — Aleksandra brings a depth of direct patient experience that shapes every policy, protocol and care plan at Eternal Life Hospice.

As an ACHC Certified Consultant and Certified Hospice Administrator, she holds the operational and clinical credentials that govern hospice quality and safety — and she leads an agency that holds Medicare certification, CDPH licensure and ACHC accreditation.

"My mission is that every patient is treated with dignity, every family feels supported and every moment of care reflects the standard I would want for my own."
— Aleksandra Dubina, Founder & CEO
The Eternal Standard

A Higher Standard of Care

Care at Eternal Life Hospice is built around The Eternal Standard — clinical confidence, guided presence, whole-person comfort and compliance-led care — with integrative comfort therapies available when appropriate, at no additional expense to the family.

It is the clinical depth of a nationally accredited agency, carried with a personal and human presence on the days that matter most. Learn about The Eternal Standard →

Approach to Care

Dignity, Family Guidance, and Whole-Person Care

Aleksandra's approach begins with a conviction that end-of-life care is not an event — it is a relationship. Every admission begins a relationship that extends to the patient, the family and the full circle of support around them.

Whole-person care at Eternal Life Hospice encompasses nursing, physician oversight, medical social work, chaplaincy, hospice aide services and integrative comfort modalities — each coordinated by a team that Aleksandra personally recruits and trains to the Eternal Standard.
