Defining Hospice Scope and Limits

Hospice election is a voluntary benefit that provides clinical, emotional and spiritual support. It is not an alternative to long-term residential facility care and room and board costs are generally not covered by the hospice benefit. Please clarify any financial questions related to your specific housing or living arrangement.

Our visits focus on managing symptoms and providing clinical education. We do not provide home health aide services for non-hospice needs, nor do we offer transportation, meal preparation or household cleaning services. These tasks remain the responsibility of the family or private caregivers engaged to support the patient's daily life.

The hospice benefit requires certification by a physician that the patient has a terminal condition with a life expectancy of six months or less if the disease runs its normal course. Eligibility is re-evaluated periodically and care continues as long as the patient meets these clinical criteria.

Common questions about hospice in Brandeis

Why do you need my exact address before discussing care?

Our hospice model is visit-based, meaning our clinical staff must travel to the patient's home. We verify your specific address to ensure our team can reliably reach your residence and provide the high-quality care that you deserve.

Does being in a specific county guarantee that you provide service?

Not automatically. While we serve many areas in Ventura County, our actual capacity is dictated by our team's ability to reach specific addresses. Some remote or difficult-to-access locations may fall outside our current service range.

What should I do if you are unable to provide service at my location?

If your home is outside our current service area, we will do our best to provide information on how to find other local resources. We recommend contacting your physician or reaching out to regional hospice agencies who may have different service logistics.
