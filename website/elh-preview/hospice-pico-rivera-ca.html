Beginning Hospice Care in Pico Rivera

Initiating hospice begins with a conversation between your family and the patient's physician regarding clinical eligibility. Certification of a terminal illness is a medical requirement, not a choice based on age or diagnosis alone. We perform an evaluation to ensure our intermittent, visit-based model aligns with the current needs of your loved one.

It is important to understand that hospice does not replace the roles of private-duty caregivers or family members who provide ongoing daily support. While we manage pain and symptoms through our clinical team, the daily physical assistance required for living remains the responsibility of the household or private arrangements.

Our team works to create a plan that supports your loved one’s comfort while clearly identifying what resources you may need to secure independently. We are available to answer your questions about how these two separate support systems function together in your home.

Medicare Benefit and Household Expenses

The Medicare hospice benefit covers specific clinical services and equipment related directly to the terminal diagnosis. This includes nursing visits, medications for pain and symptom management and essential medical supplies. It generally does not cover routine room and board, nor does it provide 24-hour custodial care or household chores.

Families often find that private expenses arise for non-hospice care needs, such as meal preparation, light housekeeping or continuous supervision. Distinguishing these costs is essential for financial planning. We encourage you to review your current insurance documentation to see which services are included under the benefit and which may require personal funding.

Please note that hospice election does not automatically end all existing treatments, but it does shift the focus to comfort. We recommend discussing all current therapies with your physician to understand what remains compatible with the hospice benefit. Our team can help you identify which expenses are likely to be managed by the benefit and which are household responsibilities.

Common questions about hospice in Pico Rivera

Does Medicare cover the costs of 24-hour home health aides?

No. The hospice benefit covers intermittent, visit-based clinical care, such as nursing and symptom management. It does not provide 24-hour supervision, private-duty caregivers or household aid. Families are responsible for arranging and funding 24-hour custodial care or domestic support if required in the home environment.

Is the hospice benefit free for all patients?

Medicare hospice covers most costs related to terminal illness, but it is not entirely free. Limited copayments may apply for specific medications or respite services. We recommend reviewing your specific insurance plan and discussing potential out-of-pocket costs with our team to ensure you understand your financial responsibilities.

Can my loved one keep seeing their primary physician?

Yes. Patients may continue to consult with their attending physician. Our hospice team coordinates directly with that doctor to integrate hospice services into the patient's care plan. We do not replace the physician but instead supplement their care with specialized, visit-based support for pain and symptom management.
