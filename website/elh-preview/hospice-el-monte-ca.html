Clinical Scope and Benefit Coverage

The hospice benefit is designed to cover services related to the terminal diagnosis, including nursing visits, physician oversight and medications used for symptom management. It is important to remember that hospice does not replace primary medical care for non-related conditions. Our team will help identify which treatments and medications fall under the hospice benefit.

Hospice services are not meant to provide continuous, 24-hour monitoring or custodial care. Our visits are intermittent and based on the clinical assessment of the patient's condition. If your loved one requires around-the-clock supervision, we can discuss how hospice services work alongside other private-pay options or facility resources you may already have in place.

We do not manage equipment delivery or routine room and board expenses. Certain copayments may apply under the Medicare benefit. We encourage families to speak with our admissions team to clarify coverage details and to ensure all questions regarding financial responsibility are addressed early in the process.

Common questions about hospice in El Monte

How do you obtain the necessary medical records for my loved one?

We coordinate directly with the patient's treating physicians. Once you authorize us to manage the referral, our clinical intake team contacts these offices to request the relevant medical history. This process ensures we have a secure, accurate foundation for creating a personalized and effective plan of care.

Should I share clinical records in the website chat?

No. Our website chat is for general inquiries only. Please use our professional referral Form to share clinical details or patient-specific information. This ensures your loved one's privacy is protected and that our intake department receives the necessary data through the appropriate, secure channels.

Is hospice care the same as 24-hour nursing?

No. Hospice care provides scheduled, visit-based services to manage symptoms and support families. It is not intended for constant, around-the-clock supervision. If the patient requires continuous care, we can help you understand how hospice services can supplement other resources you may have to ensure appropriate safety and comfort.
