The CareBidet

An automated in-bed hygiene system that helps patients who are incontinent, bedridden or mobility-challenged stay clean, dry and comfortable — with dignity, and with far less hands-on burden for the family.

Dignity in the most personal care

For families caring for a loved one who is bedbound or living with incontinence, personal hygiene is often the hardest, most frequent and most exhausting part of the day. The CareBidet — a Complete Intelligent Toileting System (CITS) developed by CoHealth Solutions — is designed to carry that burden gently and automatically.

Built-in sensors detect when the patient needs care, then the system cleans, rinses and dries — quietly and hygienically, without the constant changing of adult briefs. The result is cleaner skin, fewer disruptions to rest, and a measure of privacy and dignity that means a great deal in the days that matter most.

Comfort for the patient, relief for the caregiver

Keeping skin clean and dry isn’t only about comfort — it directly helps protect against the skin breakdown, pressure injuries and infections that incontinence can bring. By automating that care around the clock, the CareBidet helps protect skin integrity while giving exhausted family caregivers real, measurable relief.

How to arrange a CareBidet

The CareBidet is an optional device that families rent for the patient’s use at home. Eternal Life Hospice can introduce you to it, help you weigh whether it’s a good fit for your loved one’s care, and point you to CoHealth Solutions to arrange a rental.

Learn more at CoHealth Solutions ↗
Please note: The CareBidet is a third-party comfort device offered by CoHealth Solutions, not by Eternal Life Hospice. It is rented at an additional cost to the family and is not part of the Medicare hospice benefit or our included, no-cost comfort services. It is a personal-hygiene and comfort aid, not a treatment for any medical condition. Please ask our team and your physician whether it is appropriate for your loved one’s situation.
