Independent, Medicare-certified hospice brought to families throughout both counties, at home, with clinical depth and whole-person comfort.

One standard of care, across the region

Eternal Life Hospice is an independent, compliance-led agency serving every community across Ventura County and Los Angeles County. Every Medicare-certified hospice covers the same core benefit. What sets care apart is how well it is delivered, the depth of the clinical team, the reach of the comfort offered and the steadiness a family feels through it.

Our care comes to the patient wherever they call home, with a physician-led model and a nurse on call around the clock.

What we provide

✦Physician-led clinical care — with a nurse on call 24/7
✦Pain and symptom management — handled with real depth
✦Integrative comfort therapies — music, massage, reiki, aromatherapy and more
✦Emotional and spiritual support — for the patient and the whole family
✦Care wherever you live — at home, in assisted living or skilled nursing
✦Help with the Medicare benefit — explained simply, start to finish
