Common questions about hospice in Van Nuys

Does Eternal Life Hospice serve Van Nuys?

Yes. We provide Medicare-certified, ACHC-accredited hospice care to families throughout Van Nuys — at home, in assisted living, or in a skilled nursing facility. Call 805.953.7273 to speak with our team.

What parts of Van Nuys do you serve?

We serve all of Van Nuys, including Lake Balboa, Valley Glen, and Sepulveda, along with neighboring Sherman Oaks, North Hollywood, Panorama City, Reseda, and Encino.

Is hospice covered by Medicare in Van Nuys?

Yes. Medicare Part A covers hospice care in full when a physician certifies a life expectancy of six months or less — including nursing visits, comfort medications, medical equipment, aide services, social work, chaplain support, and volunteer visits, at no cost to the patient.

How quickly can care begin in Van Nuys?

We offer same-day admissions when clinically appropriate. Once a physician order is in place, our team can often begin care the same day. Call 805.953.7273.

What makes Eternal Life Hospice different in the San Fernando Valley?

Eternal Life Hospice is an independent agency with a physician-supported clinical model, a nurse on call every hour, and integrative comfort therapies — including live music, therapeutic massage, reiki, and aromatherapy — available when appropriate, at no additional expense. Our team is deeply familiar with the San Fernando Valley and coordinates with Valley Presbyterian Hospital and surrounding medical practices.
