Preparing for Your Initial Care Plan Review

During our first meetings, we listen to your loved one’s typical daily schedule to build a plan that respects their pace. We ask what times they feel most energetic, which activities offer them the most comfort and how their routine has changed as their health needs evolved.

We encourage you to observe and note these habits before our first meeting. Documenting whether they prefer mornings or afternoons for assistance helps our clinical team schedule visits that minimize disruption to their preferred rest periods.

Since clinical needs may change, we view these routines as a starting point. Your input regarding what matters most to your loved one allows us to provide care that feels personal rather than clinical, helping them remain comfortable and supported in their familiar home environment.

Understanding Coverage and Clinical Eligibility

Hospice care requires physician certification of a life-limiting condition and services are provided based on an individualized plan of care. Eligibility is determined through clinical evaluation rather than diagnosis or age alone.

While the Medicare hospice benefit covers most core services, it generally does not include routine room and board. Some copayments may apply for certain medications or treatments and not all expenses are covered. Please ask our team about what specifically relates to the hospice benefit.

Our team works with your existing physicians to coordinate care. Hospice election does not necessarily mean stopping all previous treatments and we encourage you to discuss with your primary clinician which existing treatments are essential to the patient's immediate comfort and care goals.

Common questions about hospice in Meiners Oaks

How do you incorporate existing daily habits into the hospice plan?

During our initial assessments and ongoing care reviews, we talk with the patient and family to identify preferred schedules, routines and comfort activities. We integrate these preferences into the care plan so our clinical visits support the patient's natural rhythm rather than dictating a rigid daily structure.

Will my loved one still see their own primary physician?

Yes. Our hospice team collaborates closely with the patient's existing primary physician. We keep them informed about the patient's progress and work together to ensure that the hospice plan of care is coordinated with the physician's clinical goals for the patient's comfort and ongoing symptom management.

What happens if my loved one needs care outside of a scheduled visit?

Hospice is visit-based care, not continuous or 24-hour staffing. However, we have a nurse available by phone to discuss changes in condition or urgent concerns. We will guide you on how to handle these situations, but we do not provide emergency medical triage or on-site supervision.
