AI Crawlability Improvements

The restructured architecture improves AI crawler understanding in the following ways:

  • Clear topic clusters — Hospice Care, Services, Resources are semantically distinct buckets. AI models reading the nav can infer the site's scope and authority instantly.
  • Hub → spoke linking — Each hub page links explicitly to its sub-pages. AI crawlers following internal links get a complete topic map.
  • BreadcrumbList schema — Structured data declares cluster membership in machine-readable format. Google's AI-powered Search Generative Experience uses this for attribution.
  • Descriptive anchor text — Moving from "click here" or generic labels to descriptive anchors signals topical relevance to language models indexing the site.
  • No JS-only nav — All navigation remains in crawlable anchor tags. Googlebot and AI crawlers can discover all pages without executing JavaScript.
  • /conditions/ architecture prep — Reserving the URL structure now means future condition pages slot into a pre-established cluster without orphan risk.
