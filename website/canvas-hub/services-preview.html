The Foundation

Skilled hospice care,
wherever home is

Every patient receives a coordinated, physician-directed care plan — clinical excellence and human presence woven together from the first day.

🩺

Physician & Nursing Care

Hospice-experienced physicians and skilled nurses manage advanced symptom control, respiratory care, wound care and medication coordination — in the home, around the clock.

🕐

Same-Day Admissions & 24/7 Response

When care is needed, it arrives. Our on-call nurses are available every hour of every day, with same-day admission when clinically appropriate.

🤝

Interdisciplinary Team

Physician, nurse, social worker, chaplain, certified aide and volunteer — a full circle of care around every patient and every family, coordinated under one plan.

💛

Bereavement Support

Our care does not end at the last breath. Counselors and chaplains walk with families for up to 13 months after a loss — because grief has no fixed timeline.

🛡️

Medicare & Coverage Coordination

We verify Medicare, Medi-Cal and private insurance, coordinate all covered services, and confirm costs — so families can focus on what matters.

🏠

Care in Any Setting

Home, assisted living, skilled nursing, residential board-and-care — we come to wherever the patient lives. Care moves with the patient, not the other way around.

Whole-Person Care

Integrative comfort therapies

Offered alongside the clinical care plan — at no additional expense to families — to ease the body, quiet the mind, and bring comfort to the spirit.

🎵

Music Therapy

Board-certified music therapist. Live music chosen around each patient's history and preferences.

🙌

Therapeutic Massage

Gentle, adaptive massage for comfort and circulation, modified for each patient's condition.

✨

Reiki

Non-invasive sessions for relaxation, quiet rest, and a sense of calm without demands.

🌿

Aromatherapy

Botanical essences selected to ease distress and support comfort — guided by preferences and sensitivities.

🐾

Pet Therapy

Certified therapy animals visit patients at home — especially meaningful in advanced illness.

🔔

Sound Bath

Tibetan and crystal singing bowls at the bedside — sustained tones for rest, stillness and presence.

👂

Audiology Support

Hearing assessment and support so patients can remain connected to their loved ones. Communication is care.

🌱

Holistic Medicine

Integrative approaches coordinated with the clinical team, addressing the whole person.

🕯️

End-of-Life Doula

A trained companion offering presence, legacy conversations and steady support through the final passage.

A note on these therapies: Integrative comfort modalities are offered as complementary care for ease and quality of life. They are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical condition, and they accompany — never replace — the clinical care plan. Most are provided at no additional expense to families when included in the individualized plan of care.
