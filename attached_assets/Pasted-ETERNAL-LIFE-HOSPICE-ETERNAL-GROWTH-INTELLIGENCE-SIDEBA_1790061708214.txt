ETERNAL LIFE HOSPICE
ETERNAL GROWTH INTELLIGENCE
SIDEBAR + EXECUTIVE UX SIMPLIFICATION PASS

We are NOT changing the data architecture.

We are NOT adding new features.

We are NOT changing the reporting logic.

We are simplifying the visual hierarchy so Eternal Growth Intelligence feels like a premium executive intelligence platform rather than a dense admin console.

CORE DESIGN PRINCIPLE:

ANSWER FIRST.
DETAIL SECOND.
TECHNICAL INFORMATION THIRD.

Reduce visible complexity by approximately 40%.

==================================================
5. REMOVE GOLD PILLS FROM EVERY NAV ITEM
==================================================

Current issue:

Every section appears visually highlighted because each category uses a gold capsule.

This makes all sections compete equally for attention.

Replace this treatment.

Use:

INACTIVE:
- plain cream/light text
- no gold pill
- plum background

HOVER:
- subtle tonal lift
- slightly lighter plum background

ACTIVE:
- thin gold vertical accent line OR small gold marker
- slightly brighter text
- subtle active background
- no large gold capsule

EXPANDED:
- gentle tonal container
- no gold bar spanning the full label

Gold is a premium accent.

Use it sparingly.
