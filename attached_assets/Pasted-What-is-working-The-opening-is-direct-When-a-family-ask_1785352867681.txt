What is working

The opening is direct:

“When a family asks ‘how do we choose?’ now you have an answer.”

That creates immediate relevance for case managers, discharge planners and care teams. The email also gives clear permission to share the guide with patients and caregivers which is important for distribution.

The three CTA paths are also well organized:

View the Family Guide
Submit a Patient Referral
Schedule a Staff Presentation

This supports trust-building, immediate referrals and future relationship development without turning the email into a hard sales pitch.

Changes I would make before sending
1. Change the hero wording slightly

Current:

When a family asks “how do we choose?” now you have an answer.

Better:

When a family asks “How do we choose a hospice?” you now have a clear resource to share.

The current version is emotionally strong but slightly vague. Adding “a hospice” makes the subject immediately clear.

2. Strengthen the referral CTA

Current:

Submit a Patient Referral

Use:

Refer a Patient

It is shorter, more direct and easier to process quickly.

Directly beneath it add:

For time-sensitive referrals call 805.953.7273.

That matters because urgent hospice referrals should not depend solely on a web form.

3. Improve the presentation CTA

Current:

Schedule a Staff Presentation

Use:

Request a 15-Minute Hospice Education Session

This explains what the recipient is requesting and lowers the perceived commitment.

4. Remove the fourth Aleksandra CTA

Near the signature there is another link:

Schedule a Call with Aleksandra

I would remove it from this email. There are already three primary choices. A fourth CTA creates unnecessary competition and may dilute referral clicks. Aleksandra’s phone and email are already visible.

5. Make the immediate-referral message more visible

Add this short block immediately above the three buttons:

Have a patient who may need hospice now?
Our team is available to review eligibility, answer questions and coordinate the next appropriate step throughout Ventura and Los Angeles counties.

This does not make the email aggressive. It quietly tells referral partners that Eternal is open and ready.

6. Confirm every link before sending

The visible links appear to direct to Eternal’s website but the page extraction does not expose the exact destination paths. Verify that:

Family Guide goes directly to /family-guide
Referral goes directly to /refer
Education session goes to a specific request form or scheduling page
Every link includes the planned UTM parameters
The unsubscribe link functions correctly
Final CTA section

Use this exact wording:

How Can We Support You Today?

Choose the resource or next step that is most useful for you, your team or a family in your care.

View the Family Guide

Refer a Patient

For time-sensitive referrals call 805.953.7273.

We come to you for a concise and practical discussion of hospice eligibility, the referral process and how to support families at the appropriate time.
