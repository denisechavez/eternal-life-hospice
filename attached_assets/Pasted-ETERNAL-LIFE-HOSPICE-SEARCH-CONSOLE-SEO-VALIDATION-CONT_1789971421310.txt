PHASE 3 — CLASSIFY SEARCH INTENT
==================================================

Classify Search Console queries into these groups:

A. LOCAL / COMMERCIAL HOSPICE INTENT
Examples:
- hospice Thousand Oaks
- hospice care Thousand Oaks
- hospice services Thousand Oaks
- home hospice Thousand Oaks
- in-home hospice Thousand Oaks
- hospice agency Thousand Oaks

B. FAMILY / INFORMATIONAL INTENT
Examples:
- does Medicare cover hospice
- Medicare hospice benefit
- hospice benefit periods
- what hospice covers
- hospice care benefits

C. CLINICAL / CONDITION INTENT
Examples where present:
- dementia hospice
- respiratory hospice
- pain and symptom management
- disease-specific hospice searches

D. PROFESSIONAL / REFERRAL INTENT

E. BRANDED INTENT
Examples:
- Eternal Life Hospice
- Eternal Hospice
- Aleksandra Dubina
- Eternal Life Hospice Westlake Village

F. IRRELEVANT OR ADJACENT INTENT
Examples:
- nursing homes
- generic senior care
- generic caregiving services
- assisted living
- unrelated care homes

Do not optimize Eternal Life Hospice toward an adjacent service merely because the query generated impressions.

Hospice relevance takes precedence over impression volume.

PHASE 4 — BUILD KEYWORD CLUSTERS
==================================================

Do not treat every query as a separate keyword target.

Group semantically equivalent searches into clusters.

Example:

THOUSAND OAKS CORE CLUSTER

Primary concept:
Hospice Care in Thousand Oaks

Related language may include:
- hospice Thousand Oaks
- hospice services Thousand Oaks
- home hospice Thousand Oaks
- in-home hospice Thousand Oaks
- at-home hospice Thousand Oaks
- hospice agency Thousand Oaks
- hospice nurse Thousand Oaks

These should normally support ONE strong Thousand Oaks page.

Do not create separate pages simply to target variants of the same search intent.

Apply this same semantic-clustering methodology throughout the site.
