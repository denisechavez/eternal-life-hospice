Restructure EternalLifeHospice.com into a scalable topic-based information architecture that improves:

* User experience
* SEO
* AI crawlability
* Internal linking
* Local search visibility
* Referral conversion
* Future content expansion

This is an architecture and organization project first.

Do NOT redesign the visual system.
Do NOT manually tweak individual images.
Do NOT delete existing pages.
Do NOT rename existing production URLs unless specifically approved.
Do NOT change canonicals unless specifically approved.
Do NOT remove existing schema analytics tracking forms or conversion functionality.
Do NOT create duplicate pages where relevant content already exists.

## FIRST — AUDIT BEFORE CHANGES

Before implementation:

1. Inventory every current production URL.
2. Identify current canonical URLs.
3. Identify pages included in the XML sitemap.
4. Identify current internal links.
5. Identify orphan pages.
6. Identify duplicate or overlapping content.
7. Map all existing pages into the proposed new topic structure.
8. Identify any structural change that could affect an existing URL.
9. Produce a before-and-after site architecture map.
10. Flag any proposed redirect before creating it.

Default rule:

KEEP EXISTING URLS.

Navigation hierarchy does not require URLs to change.

# 1. HOSPICE CARE

Purpose:

This is the primary educational cluster for families trying to understand hospice and make a care decision.

Structure:

* What Is Hospice Care?
* When Is It Time for Hospice?
* First 48 Hours of Hospice Care
* Hospice vs. Palliative Care
* Levels of Hospice Care
* What Hospice Covers
* Medicare Hospice Benefit

Use existing pages wherever possible.

Do not duplicate existing content.

If a relevant page already exists preserve its current URL and connect it to this hub through:

* navigation
* contextual internal links
* breadcrumbs
* sitemap relationships
* schema where appropriate

Intent distinction:

WHAT HOSPICE COVERS
= plain-language family explanation of what is generally included in hospice care.

MEDICARE HOSPICE BENEFIT
= more formal explanation of Medicare hospice eligibility benefit periods covered services and patient responsibilities.

These two pages should cross-link but should not repeat the same copy.

Review all coverage claims for accuracy before expanding them.

Do not make absolute financial claims unless fully supported.

* crawlable anchor links

* LCP
* INP
* CLS
* FCP
