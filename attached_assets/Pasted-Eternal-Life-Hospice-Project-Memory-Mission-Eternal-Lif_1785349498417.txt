Eternal Life Hospice Project Memory
Mission

Eternal Life Hospice is in a turnaround and growth phase.

Every technical decision, design decision and marketing feature must support one primary objective:

Generate qualified hospice referrals that convert into admissions.

Branding is important but measurable business growth comes first.
