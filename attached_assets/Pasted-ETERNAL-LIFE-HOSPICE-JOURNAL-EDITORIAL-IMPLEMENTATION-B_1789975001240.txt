STANDING EDITORIAL STANDARD
==================================================

Every article must:

- Have one clearly defined primary search intent
- Support its designated core page
- Avoid competing with service, resource, city, county or legacy content
- Be written for humans first
- Use natural semantic language rather than keyword repetition
- Be calm, clear, clinically responsible and useful
- Avoid generic filler
- Avoid repetitive definitions of hospice
- Avoid unsupported medical claims
- Avoid deterministic eligibility language
- Avoid coverage guarantees
- Avoid outcome claims
- Avoid "best hospice" or superiority claims
- Avoid response-time or admission-speed guarantees
- Use authoritative sources for medical, regulatory or Medicare claims when appropriate
- Preserve Eternal Life Hospice's established brand voice
- Follow Eternal's editorial convention regarding commas
- Keep JSON and staged HTML synchronized

Use Eternal's brand architecture selectively where it naturally fits:

The Eternal Standard
Clinical Confidence
Guided Presence
Whole-Person Comfort
Compliance-Led Care

These are differentiation concepts, not keywords to force into every article.
