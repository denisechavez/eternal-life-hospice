1. TYPOGRAPHY — ENLARGE AND ELEVATE
==================================================

The typography is currently too small and too delicate.

Increase type sizes across the product.

Targets:

PAGE TITLE
Make larger and more elegant.
The Executive Overview heading should feel strong and intentional.

LEAD STATEMENT
The leadership answer needs to feel important and confident.
It should be visually commanding without shouting.

PRIMARY CARD VALUES
Make materially larger.
Numbers and key states should be easy to scan instantly.

CARD LABELS
Increase size modestly.
They are too small now.

SIDEBAR LABELS
Increase size and weight slightly.
The sidebar should feel easier to scan.

SECONDARY MICROCOPY
Reduce reliance on tiny helper text.
If small text remains it should still be readable and intentional.

TYPOGRAPHIC DIRECTION

- stronger contrast between headings and secondary text
- slightly heavier body and label weights where needed
- less frail feeling
- more editorial confidence

If current fonts are too delicate in the UI then keep the brand character for headings but use a more readable supporting UI font treatment for labels navigation cards and metadata.

==================================================

The screen still feels cramped in spirit even though the structure is simplified.

Increase whitespace and padding in:

- sidebar header area
- navigation groups
- primary cards
- page header
- lower content panels
- Studio section
- Data & Settings utility

Each major block should feel more composed and less compressed.

Cards should breathe.

Panels should have more internal padding.

==================================================

==================================================

Too much tiny helper text makes the interface feel technical.

- labels under numbers
- secondary support lines
- explanatory fragments
- low-value instructions

Keep only what actually helps decision-making.

The page should not feel like it is apologizing for every metric.

Use fewer words and larger type.

==================================================

Eternal Studio must feel like a distinct world.

Create stronger distinction.

- give Studio its own stronger heading treatment
