### ADDITIONAL REQUIREMENT — INTERNAL LINKING & CONTENT UNIQUENESS

Please ensure every Eternal Journal article follows these standards:

**1. Internal Linking**
- Include 2–5 relevant internal links per article where appropriate based on length and subject matter.
- Link naturally to related Eternal Journal articles, hospice service pages, county coverage pages and educational resources.
- Use descriptive anchor text containing relevant search language when natural.
- Prioritize links that connect hospice services with our targeted geographic locations.
- Ensure every internal link leads to an active, relevant page.
- Never link to unpublished or future-scheduled articles before they become publicly available.
- Avoid excessive or repetitive linking.

**2. No Duplicate Articles**
- Audit the entire Journal before creating or modifying articles.
- Ensure every article has a distinct topic, search intent and educational purpose.
- Prevent duplicate or near-duplicate content across different cities.
- Do not create new articles covering subjects already adequately addressed unless there is a meaningful new perspective.
- Preserve all existing canonical URLs and prevent keyword cannibalization.

**3. No Duplicate Images**
- Every Journal article must have its own unique primary/hero image.
- Do not reuse the same hero image across multiple articles.
- Check the complete Journal image library before selecting or generating new photography.
- Use high-quality imagery appropriate to the article's subject and location.
- Preserve the existing image dimensions, crop settings and visual presentation.
- Include descriptive image filenames and accurate alt text.
- Avoid generic hospice stock imagery and repetitive visual compositions.

**4. Final Quality Check**

Before marking an article ready for Studio approval, verify:

- Unique article content and search intent
- Unique hero image
- Appropriate internal links
- No broken links
- No links to unpublished articles
- No duplicate SEO titles or meta descriptions
- Natural keyword integration
- Meaningful geographic relevance
- Consistent Eternal Life Hospice branding

**The objective is a connected educational content library where every article contributes something distinct while supporting the overall SEO strategy.**

Do not modify, publish or deploy anything outside the previously approved Eternal Journal and Eternal Email scope.
